CT4104 overview

CT4104 Topic Guide

X.509 certificates

Trust a public key only after validating its identity binding and chain for the intended use.

Rapid exam refresher

  • A certificate binds subject identity to public key and issuer signature; a valid signature alone is insufficient.
  • Validate chain/trust anchor, signatures, validity interval, SAN hostname, Key Usage/EKU and revocation policy.
  • Private-key possession proves control; protect, rotate and replace compromised keys.
  • Self-signed material needs an explicit trust anchor; it is not automatically trusted.

Paper evidence and scope

No local CT4104 papers exist: all practice is syllabus-based.

Medium practice — 15 marks

A browser reaches api.example.ie but gets a leaf for example.ie. (a) [5] list validation. (b) [5] explain signature limitation. (c) [5] remediate.

Answer for every part

(a)

Demand/route: use validation order. Answer: build to trusted root; verify signatures/time; match SAN api.example.ie; check serverAuth/key usage and revocation policy. Trap: checking CN only.

(b)

Demand/route: separate issuer authenticity from endpoint identity. Answer: a valid issuer signed the wrong name; it does not authenticate api.example.ie. Trap: accepting any CA-signed certificate.

(c)

Demand/route: replace identity binding safely. Answer: issue/deploy a certificate with correct SAN and its protected matching key, then test from clean trust store. Trap: disabling hostname verification.

Hard practice — 15 marks

An enterprise uses internal names and needs rapid replacement. (a) [5] choose trust model. (b) [5] lifecycle. (c) [5] stolen key response.

Answer for every part

(a)

Route: separate internal from public trust. Answer: distribute an internal CA only to managed clients. Trap: publicly trusting private names.

(b)

Route: automate short lifetime. Answer: generate keys on host/HSM, inventory certificates and renew/deploy short-lived certificates automatically. Trap: copying one key everywhere.

(c)

Route: revoke, rotate, investigate. Answer: replace/revoke the certificate, rotate related credentials and verify client trust/revocation behaviour. Trap: changing certificate without key.
