Definitions, notation and discriminating comparisons

  • Hash: digest; no origin guarantee alone.
  • MAC: shared-secret integrity/origin check.
  • Signature: private-key signing, public verification.
  • Symmetric / public-key encryption: shared-key / recipient-key confidentiality.

Step-by-step methods and solution routes

  1. Ask whether confidentiality, integrity, origin or public verification is needed.
  2. Identify shared or public/private key relationships.
  3. Select encryption, hash, MAC or signature.
  4. Explain what the construction cannot provide.

Worked examples and method extensions

Services sharing K send m with MAC(K,m). The receiver recomputes it, detecting alteration or a sender without K. It gives no public proof: either holder of K could create it.

Worked answer

Sign hash of exact artifact with protected private key; clients verify trusted public key before install. Plain hash can be swapped; MAC requires sharing a client secret.
