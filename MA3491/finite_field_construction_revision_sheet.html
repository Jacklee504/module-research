MA3491 Topic Guide

Constructing Finite Fields

A field of order pm comes from a degree-m irreducible polynomial over Fp.

Purpose

Construction questions test irreducibility, quotient notation and a usable representation for later arithmetic.Exam use: Prime p, degree m, complete irreducibility test, p^m representatives and defining reduction relation are given.

Question plan

  1. Identify p and required degree m.
  2. Test a candidate polynomial for irreducibility.
  3. Write Fp[x]/(f), define α=x mod f, and list the basis 1,α,…,αm−1.
  4. Question interpretation: For construct F_{p^m}, choose and prove irreducible a degree-m polynomial, then name residue representatives and the defining relation.

Formulas

If f is irreducible of degree m, Fp[x]/(f) has pm elements. Every element is a0+a1α+…+am−1αm−1; f(α)=0 supplies reduction.Rapid recall: F_p[x]/(f) has p^m elements and is a field exactly when f is irreducible of degree m; for degree 2 or 3, irreducible is equivalent to having no root in F_p.

Derivation

Division by f gives one unique remainder of degree below m for each coset, hence p choices for each of m coefficients. Irreducibility makes the ideal maximal, so the quotient is a field.Exam workflow: Test every base-field root when the degree permits, list all coefficient tuples, and distinguish irreducibility from primitiveness unless the latter is requested.

Worked example

Over F2, f=x3+x+1 has no roots (f(0)=f(1)=1), hence is irreducible. F8=F2[x]/(f), with α3=α+1 and elements a+bα+cα2.

Medium/hard paper-style synthesis

Construct F_9 using f(x)=x^2+1 over F_3. This fails: f(1)=2 but f(0)=1 and f(2)=4+1=2, so actually it has no root and is irreducible. Let alpha^2=-1=2. The nine elements are a+b alpha with a,b in F_3. For example (1+alpha)^2=1+2alpha+alpha^2=2alpha mod 3.

Graduated exam bridge

1. Construct F4. Check: use x2+x+1; α2=α+1.

2. Justify that x2+2 over F5 is reducible or irreducible, then construct the appropriate quotient if irreducible. Check: test roots; 2 is not a square mod 5, so irreducible.

Hard paper-style synthesis

Paper-style synthesis: explain why x^2+x+1 does not construct F_4 over F_2. Check f(1)=1+1+1=1 mod 2 and f(0)=1, so it actually has no root and is irreducible; therefore it does construct F_4. Correct the premise rather than forcing the requested conclusion.

Practice question - construct F25 [8 marks]

Inspired by: MA3491 2024/25 Q2(a-b). Task. Show x2+x+1 is irreducible over F5, then present F25. [8 marks]

Demand diagnosis and fastest route: a quadratic is irreducible iff it has no root; evaluate all five residues, then give quotient, basis and reduction relation.

Worked answer: For a=0,1,2,3,4 the values are 1,3,2,3,1 modulo 5, none zero. Thus f=x2+x+1 is irreducible. F25=F5[x]/(f). Writing α=[x], f(α)=0 gives α2=-α-1=4α+4. Every field element is uniquely a+bα with a,b∈F5, giving 52=25 elements and basis {1,α}.

Topic-specific trap: testing only 0 and 1 is sufficient over F2, not over F5.

Mistakes

  • Using a reducible modulus.
  • Writing x=α without stating the quotient.
  • Listing pm coefficients rather than pm elements.
  • Recovery rule: Evaluate roots in the base field with modular arithmetic; a question's suggested conclusion may be false and must not override the calculation.

Recent extension: characteristic-p binomial identity

2025/26 also asked: for a field F of characteristic p, show that (a+b)p=ap+bp. Treat this as a short finite-field proof extension: state the characteristic assumption explicitly and justify why the intermediate binomial coefficients vanish in F.
