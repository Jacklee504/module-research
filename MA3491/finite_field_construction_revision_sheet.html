MA3491 Topic Guide

Constructing Finite Fields

A field of order p^m comes from a degree-m irreducible polynomial over Fp.

What the local papers support

Irreducibility and quotient construction are Q2 in every 2018/19-2024/25 paper (F8,F16,F25,F27 examples).

Rapid refresher

Core ruleExam distinction
Fp[x]/(f) is a field iff f is irreducible. Its elements are degree <m representatives; f(alpha)=0 supplies reductions.For degree 2 or 3, no root proves irreducible. For higher degree, rule out factors of relevant degrees.

Medium/hard adapted practice

Paper family: Irreducibility and quotient construction are Q2 in every 2018/19-2024/25 paper (F8,F16,F25,F27 examples).

(a) Construct F8 from f=x^3+x+1 over F2. [8 marks]

Question demand: apply the definition/calculation with working. Quickest route: Test f(0),f(1); neither is zero, so the cubic has no linear factor and is irreducible. Let alpha be x mod f.

Mark-aware answer: F8=F2[alpha] with alpha^3=alpha+1. Its elements are 0,1,alpha,alpha+1,alpha2,alpha2+1,alpha2+alpha,alpha2+alpha+1.

Trap: Do not say every polynomial quotient is a field.

(b) Show x2+x+1 is irreducible over F5. [6 marks]

Question demand: Test all five field elements for roots; a quadratic is reducible iff it has a root. Quickest route: Test all five field elements for roots; a quadratic is reducible iff it has a root.

Mark-aware answer: Evaluate f(0)=1,f(1)=3,f(2)=2,f(3)=3,f(4)=1 mod 5. There is no root in F5, so the quadratic has no linear factor and is irreducible.

Trap: Use arithmetic mod 5; 5 is zero in F5.

(c) Explain why a degree-m construction has exactly pm elements. [4 marks]

Question demand: Count coefficient choices in unique degree-less-than-m representatives. Quickest route: Count coefficient choices in unique degree-less-than-m representatives.

Mark-aware answer: Every residue class has a unique representative a0+a1 alpha+...+a(m-1)alpham-1, with each ai in Fp. There are p choices for each of m coefficients, so pm elements.

Trap: Distinct polynomial expressions may represent the same class before reduction.
