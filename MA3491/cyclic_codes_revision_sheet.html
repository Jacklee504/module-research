MA3491 Topic Guide

Cyclic Codes and Generator Polynomials

Represent length-n words modulo x^n-1: multiplying by x is a cyclic shift.

What the local papers support

Cyclic generator/check-polynomial questions are Q4 in 2018/19-2021/22 and Q5 in 2022/23-2024/25.

Rapid refresher

Core ruleExam distinction
C=<g> where g divides x^n-1; k=n-deg g; h=(x^n-1)/g. Codewords are m(x)g(x), deg m<k.Rows of G are coefficient vectors of g,xg,...,x^(k-1)g; test via the check matrix/polynomial convention.

Medium/hard adapted practice

Paper family: Cyclic generator/check-polynomial questions are Q4 in 2018/19-2021/22 and Q5 in 2022/23-2024/25.

(a) For a length-5 code over F4 with deg g=2, give dimension and generator-matrix route. [8 marks]

Question demand: apply the definition/calculation with working. Quickest route: Use k=5-2=3; write g,xg,x2g coefficient rows in the stated coordinate order.

Mark-aware answer: The code has dimension 3. Its generator matrix is formed from coefficient vectors of g, xg and x2g (reduced modulo x5-1 if needed). Compute h=(x5-1)/g before constructing the check matrix.

Trap: Do not reverse coefficient order halfway through the answer.

(b) Derive the check polynomial and explain the membership test. [6 marks]

Question demand: Factor xn-1=g(x)h(x); a word is in C iff its polynomial is divisible by g. Quickest route: Factor xn-1=g(x)h(x); a word is in C iff its polynomial is divisible by g.

Mark-aware answer: Given g dividing xn-1, set h=(xn-1)/g. A length-n word c(x) is a codeword precisely when g divides c in Fq[x] (equivalently it has zero syndrome under the associated check matrix). Show the division/remainder.

Trap: Do not divide in integers; coefficients live in Fq.

(c) Explain why multiplying a codeword by x gives a cyclic shift. [4 marks]

Question demand: Work modulo xn-1 and track the xn term. Quickest route: Work modulo xn-1 and track the xn term.

Mark-aware answer: If c(x)=c0+c1x+...+c(n-1)xn-1, then xc(x)=c(n-1)+c0x+...+c(n-2)xn-1 modulo xn-1. This is a one-place cyclic shift and remains a multiple of g.

Trap: The coordinate order convention determines shift direction.
