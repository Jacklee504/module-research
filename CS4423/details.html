Module Summary

CS4423 has a largely written, mathematical Network Science exam. Its core question families repeat well across the local archive, particularly BFS, centrality, random graphs, graph representation and small-world networks. The official weighting is 80% written assessment and 20% continuous assessment.

The most recent 2025/26 paper retained the seven compulsory-question format introduced in 2024/25, despite a change in the starred internal examiner. That supports the current structure, but not an exact specialist-topic prediction.

Official Module Information

Source: University of Galway CS4423 module page. The page is valid from 2025 onwards.

  • Semester 2, 5 credits, introduction to Network Science.
  • Written Assessment: 80%.
  • Continuous Assessment: 20%.
  • Official outcomes: graph-theoretic network description, traversal, centrality, random graph generators, markets as networks, and document networks such as the web.
Outcome Coverage Caveat

The papers strongly support graph theory, BFS, centrality, random graphs, and small-world networks. Markets and document networks are still official scope, but are historical rather than recent-paper priorities. Keep them in coverage and let current lectures, tutorials, and CA set their final weighting.
