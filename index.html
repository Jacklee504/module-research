Rough Study Plan

Current Week 22-28 August, 70% Semester 1 / 30% Semester 2 Open guide

Semester 1: CT417, CT4101, CT4100, CT561 and CT4111. Semester 2: CT4104, CS402, CT421, MA3491 and CS4423.

  1. 22-28 AugustCurrent week

    Close the internship phase

    Weekend, 7-8h S1: Split the time between CT417 architecture, testing or CI/CD, CT4101 evaluation and practical workflow, and one selected option: CT4100, CT561 or CT4111. Finish each block with a short starting sheet or active-recall prompt.

    Weekend, 3-4h S2: Use one focused block for CS402 or CT421, then consolidate CT4104 foundations. Do not open a new major topic.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Turn your S1 notes into a short list of first-week questions and prerequisites to resolve.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Plan the post-internship gap: assign CT417, CT4101, CT4100, CT561 and CT4111 their first focused session.

17-28 August

Transition Toward Semester 1

While the internship is still running, move to 70% Semester 1 and 30% Semester 2. Aim for recognition and confidence, not exam-level mastery.

29 August-Term starts

Core Semester 1 Prep

Use the post-internship gap for concentrated S1 preparation. Keep only a short S2 recall block so the summer work remains available.

October-December

Deliver Semester 1

CA and the project take priority. Keep roughly one light S2 touchpoint each fortnight, then reserve the final weeks for repeated paper topics.

January

Switch To Semester 2

Use the break to start Semester 2 foundations. Keep CT413 moving, but move the main study block toward CT4104, CS402, CT421, MA3491 and CS4423.

February-March

Finish CA And Project

This is the highest-value period for bankable marks. Protect CT413 completion time and do not let predictable exams pull focus from assessed work.

April-May

Convert Patterns Into Marks

Use papers as timed practice. Start with the modules whose patterns are strongest, then spend remaining time on weak required material.

Semester rule: from now until 28 August, use 70% of preparation time on Semester 1 and 30% on light Semester 2 preparation. From 29 August until teaching starts, use the available time for concentrated Semester 1 core preparation while retaining one short Semester 2 recall session. Reverse the balance in January while keeping a protected CT413 block through completion.
