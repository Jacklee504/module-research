Rough Study Plan

Summer Split

Semester 2 first, then Semester 1 leads

Now to 16 August 10-12h each weekend Across both days: 6-7h S2 and 4-5h S1. Make Semester 2 familiar before teaching and CT413 compete for attention.
17-28 August 10-12h each weekend Across both days: 7-8h S1 and 3-4h S2. Shift toward the immediate semester while retaining one light S2 touchpoint.
Two weekdays, mandatory 2 x 75-90 minutes Use these for recall, exercises and notes from the weekend, not a second full weekend workload.
Next Five Weeks 13 July to 16 August, 40% Semester 1 / 60% Semester 2 Open guide

Semester 1: CT417, CT4101, CT4100, CT561 and MA4102. Semester 2: CT4104, CS402, CT421, MA3491 and MA342. Use these as the concrete focus for the S1 and S2 blocks below.

  1. Week 113-19 July

    Choose the runway

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Spend about 3h mapping CT4104, including networking, TLS and likely CA-style work. Spend another 3-4h mapping CS402 or CT421, identifying prerequisites and completing its first short tutorial.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Refresh CT4101 model evaluation for about 2h, then use 2-3h on a CT417 architecture or testing concept.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Write down the exact S1 and S2 modules, then record the topics that felt most interesting or difficult.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Recreate the CT4104 and CS402/CT421 maps from memory, then fill only the gaps.

  2. Week 220-26 July

    Start the Semester 2 foundation

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Complete a focused foundation tutorial for CT421 or MA3491, then spend a second block doing exercises, examples or a second tutorial. Keep notes to one page of definitions, methods and weak points.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Do a small CT4101 practical or ML recap, then use the remaining time on CT4100, CT561 or MA4102.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Recall the S2 material without notes and fill only the gaps you find.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Complete a small ML or S2 exercise and update the one-page note with the mistake you made.

  3. Week 327 July-2 August

    Build a second S2 thread

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Split the time between CT4104 fundamentals and the next topic in your selected S2 module. Keep this exploratory, not exam revision, and finish each block with worked examples or active recall.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Complete one foundation tutorial for CT4100, CT561 or MA4102, then reinforce CT417 design and testing or CT4101 practical skills.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Make five flashcards, worked examples or prompts from the week’s two hardest concepts.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Explain one selected S2 topic in writing without notes, then correct it using the source material.

  4. Week 43-9 August

    Turn familiarity into recall

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Revisit the two S2 areas already covered, produce a concise concept map, then use a second block for exercises or worked examples. Include links between CT4104 and any AI, crypto, maths or systems option you selected.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Focus on CT417 CI/CD, testing or architecture, then use a second block on CT4101, CT4100, CT561 or MA4102. Capture the terms you would want to recognise in week one.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Check the selected module pages and record the first topic to revisit once teaching begins.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Rebuild the S2 concept map and add only the links you can justify from your notes.

  5. Week 510-16 August

    Close the S2-led block

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Do one more focused tutorial or worked-problem session for CS402, CT421, MA3491 or MA342. Use the second block to consolidate CT4104, then finish with a one-page starting sheet for term.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Create a matching starting sheet for CT4101, CT417, CT4100, CT561 and MA4102, then use any remaining time to close the weakest prerequisite you identified.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Plan the following two weeks at 70% S1 / 30% S2 and decide the fixed weekend slots you will protect.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Revisit the weakest topic from the five-week block and write the next concrete resource or exercise to use.

July-16 August

Set Up The Year

Use 40% of the time on Semester 1 and 60% on Semester 2. Make the later semester familiar now, without trying to cover whole modules.

17-28 August

Transition Toward Semester 1

While the internship is still running, move to 70% Semester 1 and 30% Semester 2. Aim for recognition and confidence, not exam-level mastery.

29 August-Term starts

Core Semester 1 Prep

Use the post-internship gap for concentrated S1 preparation. Keep only a short S2 recall block so the summer work remains available.

October-December

Deliver Semester 1

CA and the project take priority. Keep roughly one light S2 touchpoint each fortnight, then reserve the final weeks for repeated paper topics.

January

Switch To Semester 2

Use the break to start the S2 option with the best CA fit. Keep CT413 moving, but move the main study block toward S2 foundations.

February-March

Finish CA And Project

This is the highest-value period for bankable marks. Protect CT413 completion time and do not let predictable exams pull focus from assessed work.

April-May

Convert Patterns Into Marks

Use papers as timed practice. Start with the modules whose patterns are strongest, then spend remaining time on weak required material.

Semester rule: until 16 August, use 40% of preparation time on Semester 1 and 60% on Semester 2. From 17-28 August, use 70% on Semester 1 and 30% on light Semester 2 preparation. From 29 August until teaching starts, use the available time for concentrated Semester 1 core preparation while retaining one short Semester 2 recall session. Reverse the balance in January while keeping a protected CT413 block through completion.
