Next Five Weeks 13 July to 16 August, 40% Semester 1 / 60% Semester 2 Open guide

Your module choices will appear here once added to the planner above.

  1. Week 113-19 July

    Choose the runway

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Spend about 3h mapping CT4104, including networking, TLS and likely CA-style work. Spend another 3-4h mapping one likely S2 option, identifying prerequisites and completing its first short tutorial.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Refresh CT4101 model evaluation for about 2h, then use 2-3h on a CT417 architecture or testing concept.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Add likely modules above and write down what felt most interesting or difficult.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Recreate the CT4104 and S2-option maps from memory, then fill only the gaps.

  2. Week 220-26 July

    Start the Semester 2 foundation

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Complete a focused foundation tutorial for your main S2 option, then spend a second block doing exercises, examples or a second tutorial. Keep notes to one page of definitions, methods and weak points.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Do a small CT4101 practical or ML recap, then use the remaining time on the likely S1 option or CT417 if that choice is not settled.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Recall the S2 material without notes and fill only the gaps you find.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Complete a small ML or S2 exercise and update the one-page note with the mistake you made.

  3. Week 327 July-2 August

    Build a second S2 thread

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Split the time between CT4104 fundamentals and the next topic in your selected S2 module. Keep this exploratory, not exam revision, and finish each block with worked examples or active recall.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Complete one foundation tutorial for your likely S1 option, then reinforce CT417 design and testing or CT4101 practical skills.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Make five flashcards, worked examples or prompts from the week’s two hardest concepts.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Explain one selected S2 topic in writing without notes, then correct it using the source material.

  4. Week 43-9 August

    Turn familiarity into recall

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Revisit the two S2 areas already covered, produce a concise concept map, then use a second block for exercises or worked examples. Include links between CT4104 and any AI, crypto, maths or systems option you selected.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Focus on CT417 CI/CD, testing or architecture, then use a second block on CT4101 or the likely S1 option. Capture the terms you would want to recognise in week one.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Check the module pages and adjust your choice only if topic fit or assessment weighting now changes the decision.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Rebuild the S2 concept map and add only the links you can justify from your notes.

  5. Week 510-16 August

    Close the S2-led block

    Weekend, 6-7h S2: Do one more focused tutorial or worked-problem session for the S2 option most likely to be chosen. Use the second block to consolidate CT4104, then finish with a one-page starting sheet for term.

    Weekend, 4-5h S1: Create a matching starting sheet for CT4101, CT417 and the likely S1 option, then use any remaining time to close the weakest prerequisite you identified.

    Weekday 1, 75-90m: Plan the following two weeks at 70% S1 / 30% S2 and decide the fixed weekend slots you will protect.

    Weekday 2, 75-90m: Revisit the weakest topic from the five-week block and write the next concrete resource or exercise to use.
