Rapid exam refresher

Use names, units, equations and initials; validate structure, dimensions, extremes and behaviour before fitting. Policy analysis compares baseline/plausible scenarios with sensitivity and side effects, not a single plot.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before application.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Validate and compare disease policies. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: validate progressively. Quickest route: structure/units, extremes, pattern, calibration/holdout. Answer: review with experts, units-check, test zero transmission, compare behaviour and holdout. Trap: fit proves causality.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: compare uncertainty. Quickest route: common baseline, metrics, sensitivity, trade-off. Answer: compare peak, cumulative cost/cases and recovery over compliance/delay ranges. Trap: choose from one run.
