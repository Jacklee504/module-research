Rapid exam refresher

SIR: dS/dt=-IR, dI/dt=IR-RR, dR/dt=RR; IR=beta SI/N, RR=I/D, R0=beta D. Herd threshold=1-1/R0. Quarantine/vaccination split flows and preserve population accounting.

Adapted exam practice - Design SIR with countermeasures. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: preserve flows/equations. Quickest route: infection -> I/Q split -> recovery -> vaccination S to V. Answer: use beta SI/N, route QF to Q and remaining to I, recover after D and vaccinate S. Trap: quarantined infect if excluded.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: compare outcomes. Quickest route: mixing versus susceptible supply, attack rate. Answer: quarantine lowers mixing, vaccination lowers S; attack rate is cumulative ever infected/N. Trap: current I/N is attack rate.
