Lecture to exam route

Read each paper’s stated initial values, time step and constraints before calculating. Attempt the multipart construction, then reveal its plan.

  • Graphical integration

    2025/26 Q1(a) [12], 2024/25 Q4(a) [12]: from inflow and outflow graphs, draw the stock-flow structure, calculate net flow and trace the stock.

    Reveal answer plan
    • Net flow = total inflows − total outflows, with units of stock per time.
    • The stock changes by the signed area under net flow. Positive, zero and negative net flow make it rise, stay level or fall.
    • For each segment, state slope and curvature, then carry the previous stock value forward; respect the stated initial value and any permission for negative stock.

  • Euler integration

    2025/26 Q1(b) [5] and Q1(c) [8]: state the within-step assumption, update the stock and justify whether the estimate is high or low.

    Reveal answer plan
    • S(t + Δt) = S(t) + Δt × net flow at the step start.
    • Euler treats that sampled rate as constant across the step. Compare each rectangle with the actual area under the net-flow curve to decide error direction.
    • Show each step, its rate, area and running stock using exactly the Δt requested.

  • Fractional rates

    2025/26 Q2(a) [8], 2024/25 Q2(a) [6]: model a stock with a fractional decrease rate approaching a goal, and explain its behaviour and units.

    Reveal answer plan
    • Outflow = stock × decrease fraction. The fraction has units 1/time.
    • Make the fraction a state: its change rate is (goal − current fraction)/adjustment time. State its initial value.
    • As the rate approaches its goal, explain the changing outflow and stock trajectory with a plausible real setting.

  • Feedback and backlog

    2025/26 Q3(a)–(c) [6, 12, 7]: construct a backlog stock, sign its links and trace policy and side-effect loops.

    Reveal answer plan
    • Backlog accumulates new work plus rework minus completed work.
    • For every link, state whether increasing its cause increases or decreases its effect, all else equal.
    • Trace each closed loop from backlog back to backlog. Raising completion can balance the backlog; pressure that reduces quality and adds rework can reinforce it.
    Canvas feedback2025/26 paperQuestion family
  • Stock management

    2025/26 Q4(b)–(d) [5, 10, 5]: distinguish expected losses from target adjustment and explain a delayed hiring response.

    Reveal answer plan
    • Required inflow = expected normal losses + (desired stock − actual stock)/adjustment time.
    • Expected losses replace routine outflow; the gap term moves the stock toward its goal. A smoothing delay makes expectations respond gradually.
    • For an ageing-chain question, draw every staff stock and progression or attrition flow before equations. Explain any lower bound on hiring when the target falls.
    Canvas stock management2025/26 paperQuestion family
  • Delays

    Lecture 6 extends stock-flow modelling to first- and second-order material delays, pipeline delays and information smoothing. Use the paper's specified construction when a delayed progression or expectation appears.

    Reveal answer plan
    • For a first-order material delay, accumulate material in transit and divide that stock by average delay time to obtain outflow.
    • For a second-order delay, use two transit stocks in series and split the average delay time across their outflows. A pipeline delay instead preserves order and gives each item the same transit time.
    • For an information delay, make perceived value a stock adjusted by (reported value − perceived value)/adjustment time; distinguish this perception from a material flow.
    Canvas delays lecture2025/26 paper
  • SIR models and policy

    The SIR construction appears in all three current papers. In 2025/26 Q2(b) [12], add quarantine of 20% of infection flow, vaccination, a goal-seeking vaccination fraction, attack rate and feedbacks; Q2(c) [5] asks for a bubble plot.

    Reveal answer plan
    • Write force of infection λ = βI/N, infection flow λS, and recovery flow I/D, with people per time as flow units.
    • For this paper, split the infection flow into quarantine 0.2λS and infectious 0.8λS; do not use the different 2024/25 construction that quarantines existing infectious people.
    • Move susceptible people to Vaccinated through a vaccination flow, define the goal-seeking fraction, then label final attack rate and the two bubble-plot axes.
