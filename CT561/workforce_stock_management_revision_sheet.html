What the local papers show

Rapid exam refresher

Stock management combines anchor (expected replacement) and adjustment=(desired-current)/AT. Workforce can progress trainee->experienced->senior with attrition; target changes alter adjustment while anchor maintains steady state.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before application.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Model staged pharmaceutical staffing. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: combine pipeline/target feedback. Quickest route: stocks, progression/attrition, gap/AT, anchor+adjustment. Answer: define stages/delays/fractional attrition; hire equals anchor plus target-gap adjustment. Trap: progression changes total staff.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: explain target-reduction response. Quickest route: negative gap -> lower hiring -> attrition lowers stock -> equilibrium. Answer: bound hires at zero if needed; AT controls speed/overshoot. Trap: negative hire without layoffs.
