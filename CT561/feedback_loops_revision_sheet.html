CT561 Paper-Based Topic Guide

Feedback Loops and Workload Policies

Recent papers repeatedly turn a verbal workload scenario into a signed diagram, named loops and a policy judgement.

Repeated recent-paper structure

  • Define and classify [5-6]: link polarity, loop polarity, feedback, balancing and reinforcing behaviour.
  • Build the model [10-12]: one backlog stock, stated flows/auxiliaries, link signs and stock equation where requested.
  • Analyse policy loops [7-8]: name each loop, trace an increase around it, calculate polarity, then discuss unintended effects.

References: 2023/24 Q1(b,c), 2024/25 Q1(a-c), 2025/26 Q3(a-c). Earlier close variants: 2016/17 Q1, 2018/19 Q2 and 2019/20 Q2.

Definitions that earn the short-question marks

  • Link polarity: a positive link means variables move in the same direction; a negative link means they move in opposite directions, other things equal.
  • Feedback loop: a closed causal chain in which a variable eventually affects itself.
  • Loop polarity: an even number of negative links gives reinforcing feedback; an odd number gives balancing feedback.
  • Balancing loop: counters a change or closes a gap. Reinforcing loop: amplifies a change.

Always confirm with an increase-trace. For Backlog +> Pressure +> Completed -> Backlog, an increase in backlog eventually causes a decrease in backlog. One negative link means balancing.

2025/26 claims-backlog model [12]

Use the exact structure from 2025/26 Q3(b):

Claims Backlog = INTEG(New Claims + Reworked Claims - Claims Completed, Initial Backlog)

  • Claims Backlog + Work Pressure.
  • Pressure - Time per Claim; lower Time per Claim + Claims Completed.
  • Pressure + Temporary Staff; Temporary Staff + Claims Completed.
  • Pressure + Workweek; Workweek + Claims Completed.
  • Lower Time per Claim + Reworked Claims.
  • Temporary Staff + Time per Claim.
  • Workweek + Fatigue; Fatigue - Claims Completed.

Draw flows as pipes into/out of the stock. Pressure, time per claim, temporary staff, workweek and fatigue are auxiliaries with information arrows, not material flows.

How to answer the discussion part

Do not say simply that overtime or temporary staff is good or bad. State that the immediate policy loops are balancing, but the quality, training and fatigue side-effects reinforce backlog. Delays make a policy look effective at first and harmful later. Recommend testing a combined policy that protects quality and sustainable capacity, and name the outcome measures: backlog, completion rate, rework and fatigue.

Mark checklist

  • Every stated variable appears once and every link has a sign.
  • Flows connect to the stock; information links do not.
  • Each requested loop is named, closed and traced.
  • Explain the management trade-off, including any delay.
