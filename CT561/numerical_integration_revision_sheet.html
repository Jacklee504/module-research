Rapid exam refresher

Stock(t+DT)=Stock(t)+NetFlow(t)DT; net flow=inflows-outflows. Graphical integration adds signed area. Euler assumes start-step flow constant: rising linear flow underestimates, falling overestimates; smaller DT reduces error.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before application.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Integrate a varying tank flow. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: transform flow to stock slope. Quickest route: sign, zero crossing, signed area. Answer: stock rises with positive net flow, peaks at zero, falls with negative and curves as flow changes. Trap: copy flow curve as stock.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: explain Euler error. Quickest route: new=old+start flow DT; compare area. Answer: rising flow makes the left rectangle low, falling high; use net, not one flow. Trap: omit outflow.
