Exam topic guide

Stock-and-Flow Modelling

Stocks accumulate; flows change stocks/time; auxiliaries/information links determine rates.

What the local papers show

Stock-flow construction is central to 2024/25 Q1(b) and 2025/26 Q3(b), underpinning integration questions.

Rapid exam refresher

Stocks accumulate; flows change stocks/time; auxiliaries/information links determine rates. Write Stock=INTEG(inflows-outflows,initial). Check units and separate physical flows from causal links.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before application.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Model assignment backlog. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: convert prose to conservation. Quickest route: stock, inflows/outflow, INTEG, rates. Answer: Backlog=INTEG(Allocated+Rework-Completed,initial), each flow assignments/week. Trap: pressure as physical flow.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: prove signs/units. Quickest route: annotate links, trace loop, stock/time check. Answer: test zero capacity and ensure every flow shares units/time. Trap: add mismatched time units.
