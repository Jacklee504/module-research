1. Graphical and Euler Integration

FrontBackEvidence
What is the stock equation for one inflow and one outflow?Stock = INTEG(Inflow - Outflow, Initial Stock). Net flow is Inflow - Outflow; it has units stock/time, while Stock has units stock.Current Q4/Q1 integration
How is a stock change found by graphical integration on a linear net-flow interval?change in stock = ((NF_start + NF_end)/2) x elapsed time. This is the signed trapezoid area under net flow; add it to the starting stock.Current Q4/Q1 integration
How does net-flow sign determine stock behaviour?Positive net flow means the stock rises; negative net flow means it falls; zero net flow gives a horizontal tangent. If positive net flow is falling, stock rises at a decreasing rate; if negative net flow is falling, stock falls at an increasing rate.Current Q4/Q1 integration
Where are stock maxima and minima on a net-flow graph?A stock maximum occurs where net flow crosses from positive to negative. A stock minimum occurs where net flow crosses from negative to positive. Net flow equal to zero without a sign change gives a flat point, not necessarily an extremum.Current Q4/Q1 integration
State forward Euler integration.S(t+DT) = S(t) + NF(t) x DT, where NF(t) is the net flow at the start of the interval. It approximates the integral by a left-endpoint rectangle.Current Q4/Q1 integration
What assumptions underlie forward Euler in CT561 answers?Net flow is treated as constant during each DT; therefore the stock is approximated as changing linearly over that step; DT must be small enough that this is an acceptable approximation.Current Q4/Q1 integration
When does forward Euler overestimate or underestimate the true stock change for linear net flow?It overestimates when net flow falls, because each left-endpoint rectangle is above the line. It underestimates when net flow rises, because each left-endpoint rectangle is below the line.2023/24, 2024/25, 2025/26 Euler theory
Why is the end-of-step net flow not used in a forward Euler update?An update for [t, t+DT] uses NF(t). The value at t+DT begins the next interval; it is not part of the current left-endpoint rectangle.Current Euler calculations
Give the 2025/26 Euler-error support calculation on days 0-2.5.With DT=0.5, start-of-step net flows are 50, 40, 30, 20, 10. Euler change is (50+40+30+20+10)x0.5 = 75. The true triangular area is 0.5x2.5x50=62.5, so Euler overestimates by 12.5 units.2025/26 Q1(b)
What table should a full Euler calculation show?One row per DT interval with time/interval, current or starting net flow, NF x DT, and next stock. Preserve units and use the stated DT, even when it is deliberately large.Current Q4/Q1(c)

2. Feedback, Workload and Policy Loops

FrontBackEvidence
Define positive and negative causal-link polarity.A positive link means an increase in the cause tends to increase the effect, and a decrease tends to decrease it, all else equal. A negative link means an increase in the cause tends to decrease the effect, and vice versa.2024/25 Q1, 2025/26 Q3
Define feedback and loop polarity.Feedback is a closed causal chain in which a variable eventually affects itself. Count negative links around the closed loop: an even number gives reinforcing polarity; an odd number gives balancing polarity. Confirm it with an increase-trace.2023/24-2025/26 workload questions
Distinguish reinforcing and balancing feedback.Reinforcing feedback amplifies an initial change, for example more infectious people cause more infections. Balancing feedback counteracts a change or closes a gap, for example backlog raises completions that reduce backlog.2024/25 Q1(a), 2025/26 Q3(a)
Why add feedback structures to a stock-flow model?They reveal how flows are causally generated, expose delayed side-effects and policy resistance, and explain behaviour over time rather than treating rates as independent inputs.2024/25 Q1(a)
What is the stock equation for a workload backlog with new work, rework and completion?Backlog = INTEG(New Work + Rework - Completed, Initial Backlog). New Work and Rework are material inflows; Completed is a material outflow. Pressure, fatigue and workweek are auxiliaries unless the paper explicitly makes them stocks.2024/25 Q1(b), 2025/26 Q3(b)
Prove that a workload capacity policy is balancing.Trace a rise in Backlog: Backlog + -> Pressure + -> policy effort/capacity + -> Completed + -> Backlog -. The final change reduces the original backlog, and there is one negative link, so the loop is balancing.Current workload loop parts
Why is the shorter-time-per-task rework loop reinforcing?Backlog + -> Pressure + -> Time per Task - -> Rework + -> Backlog +. There are two negative links, so the initial rise in Backlog is amplified. State that lower time can reduce quality and create later rework.2018/19, 2024/25, 2025/26 variants
Why is the workweek-fatigue loop reinforcing?Backlog + -> Pressure + -> Workweek + -> Fatigue + -> Completed - -> Backlog -. Two negative links give reinforcing polarity. A delay in fatigue makes the policy look helpful before it worsens backlog.2018/19, 2023/24-2025/26
Why can temporary staff create a reinforcing side-effect in the 2025/26 claims model?Backlog + -> Pressure + -> Temporary Staff + -> Time per Claim + -> Completed - -> Backlog -. Two negative links make the loop reinforcing. The stated mechanism is that more temporary staff increases time per claim.2025/26 Q3(b,c)
What makes a workload-policy discussion exam-safe?Name the intended balancing loop and each delayed reinforcing side-effect; state the mechanism and affected outcome; then recommend testing a combined policy against backlog, completion, rework, fatigue and capacity rather than asserting that overtime or hiring is always best.Current workload discussion parts

3. Fractional Rates, Stock Management and Workforce

FrontBackEvidence
Define a fractional increase flow with units.Increase = Stock x Fractional Increase Rate. The fraction has units 1/time, so Increase has units stock/time. With a constant positive fraction and no opposing flow, the stock shows exponential growth.2021/22 Q1(a), 2024/25 Q2(a)
Define a fractional decrease flow with units.Decrease = Stock x Fractional Decrease Rate. The fraction has units 1/time; the outflow has units stock/time. With a constant fraction and no inflow, the stock decays exponentially.2021/22 Q1(a), 2024/25 Q2(a)
Define adjustment to a goal.Adjustment = (Goal - Actual)/Adjustment Time. It is positive below goal, negative above goal and zero at goal. It is a balancing feedback structure, and Adjustment Time has time units.2019/20 Q3(a), 2024/25 Q2(a)
Give the first-order smoothing structure and explain AT.Smoothed = INTEG((Input - Smoothed)/AT, Initial Smoothed). It is an adjustment-to-goal stock with Input as the goal. Larger AT produces a slower, smoother response; smaller AT tracks Input more closely.2023/24 Q2(a)
How is the 2025/26 varying fractional decrease structure constructed?Make the decrease fraction a goal-seeking state: G = INTEG((G* - G)/AT, G0), then use Outflow = S x G. G* is the maximum/target fraction and AT controls how quickly the rate changes.2025/26 Q2(a)
Distinguish the anchor from the adjustment in stock management.The anchor replaces expected normal losses; the adjustment corrects the current desired-actual gap. A simple goal-seeking model has only the adjustment. Stock management combines both, so it can stay at target despite expected losses.2023/24 Q2(b), 2024/25 Q2(b), 2025/26 Q4(b)
What equations express an anchor and total required inflow?Expected Losses = SMOOTH(Actual Losses, Expectation Adjustment Time) and Required Inflow = Expected Losses + (Desired Stock - Actual Stock)/Adjustment Time. At equilibrium, adjustment is zero and required inflow equals expected losses.Stock management current papers
How is desired University staff calculated?Desired Staff = Students / Desired Student-Staff Ratio. Students are the numerator and the desired ratio is the denominator. For 10,000 students and ratio 20, desired staff is 500.2018/19 onward; 2024/25 Q2(c)
Give a complete core University staff-recruitment model.Staff = INTEG(Hires - Quits, Staff0); Quits = Quit Fraction x Staff; Expected Quits = SMOOTH(Quits, Expectation Adjustment Time); Hires = Expected Quits + (Desired Staff - Staff)/Staff Adjustment Time; Desired Staff = Students/Desired Ratio.2024/25 Q2(c)
What happens when the desired student-to-staff ratio falls from 20 to 10 with Students fixed at 10,000?Desired Staff rises from 10,000/20=500 to 10,000/10=1,000. The discrepancy becomes positive, hires exceed quits, and Staff rises towards 1,000 at a decreasing rate. Staff Adjustment Time most directly controls settling speed.2018/19 Q4(c), 2024/25 Q2(c)
Give the three-stage ageing-chain workforce equations.Trainees=INTEG(Hires-Progress TE-Attrition T,T0); Experienced=INTEG(Progress TE-Progress ES-Attrition E,E0); Seniors=INTEG(Progress ES-Attrition S,S0). Use Progress TE=Trainees/Delay TE, Progress ES=Experienced/Delay ES, stage-specific fractional attrition, and Total Staff=Trainees+Experienced+Seniors.2017/18 Q4(d), 2025/26 Q4(c)

4. SIR, Proofs and Public-Health Extensions

FrontBackEvidence
Give the core frequency-dependent SIR equations.lambda = beta I/N; Infections=lambda S=beta I S/N; Recoveries=I/D; S=INTEG(-Infections,S0); I=INTEG(Infections-Recoveries,I0); R=INTEG(Recoveries,R0). In a closed model, N=S+I+R is constant.All SIR papers
Relate the c, i, d notation to beta and R0.c is contacts per infectious person per time, i is transmission probability per contact, and d is mean infectious duration. beta=c i and R0=beta d=c i d.2018/19 Q3, 2023/24 Q3(a)
Define R0 and effective reproduction number.R0 is the expected number of secondary infections caused by one infectious person in a wholly susceptible population under the model assumptions. In the basic SIR model, Re=R0(S/N); infections grow when Re>1.2018/19 onward
Calculate lambda for N=10,000, 2% infectious, R0=12 and D=7.beta=R0/D=12/7 per day; I/N=0.02; therefore lambda=beta I/N=(12/7)(0.02)=0.0343 per day. This is per susceptible per day, not total new infections.2024/25 Q3(a)
State and calculate the herd-immunity threshold in the basic SIR model.HIT = 1 - 1/R0. For R0=12, HIT=1-1/12=11/12=0.9167, or about 91.7%. It is the immune fraction needed under the model assumptions to make initial Re at most 1.2016/17, 2019/20, 2024/25
Give the outbreak-threshold proof in a wholly susceptible population.Start from dI/dt = beta I S/N - I/D. With S=N and I>0, growth requires beta - 1/D > 0, hence beta D > 1. With beta=c i and D=d, this is c i d > 1. The equilibrium threshold is c i d=1.2017/18 Q3(a), 2023/24 Q3(a)
Prove that S/N=1/R0 at the infection peak.At a peak, dI/dt=0, so beta I S/N=I/D. For I>0, divide by I: beta S/N=1/D. Multiply by D and substitute R0=beta D: R0 S/N=1; therefore S/N=1/R0.2018/19 Q3(c)
Name and classify the three standard SIR feedback loops.Contagion is reinforcing: I raises Infections, which raises I. Depletion is balancing: I raises Infections, which lowers S and therefore lowers Infections. Recovery is balancing: I raises Recoveries, which lowers I.2018/19 Q3(b)
How is quarantine modelled in the 2024/25 SIR question?Keep core SIR, add Quarantine, and move a stated fraction of Infectious people to it, controlled by a 0 or 1 policy flag. Quarantined people recover at the same rate as I. State exactly whether Q transmits; do not assume it without the prompt.2024/25 Q3(b)
How is quarantine modelled differently in 2025/26?Split the infection flow itself: To Q=QF x lambda x S and To I=(1-QF) x lambda x S. Q receives new infections immediately, recovers at Q/2, and does not infect others. This differs from moving people later from I to Q.2025/26 Q2(b)
How is goal-seeking vaccination added in 2025/26?Add Vaccinated stock and Vaccinations=Vaccination Fraction x S. Make the fraction change by Vaccination Fraction=INTEG((0.15-Vaccination Fraction)/AT,0). Vaccination removes only S, so Vaccinated people are not left in the infection flow.2025/26 Q2(b)
Define overall attack rate for the 2025/26 model.Attack Rate=(I+Q+R)/N when I and Q are currently infected and R contains recovered infected people. Vaccinated people are excluded because they were not infected.2025/26 Q2(b)
How should an SIR model be modified for cohorts such as young and elderly?Replicate the disease states by cohort, for example Sy,Iy,Ry and Se,Ie,Re. Give each group its own infection and recovery flows; use within- and between-cohort contact terms or a contact matrix if groups mix. Sum all cohort stocks for total N.2016/17 Q3(c), 2024/25 Q3(c)
What must the 2023/24 SEI2H2R construction include?S to Exposed infection flow; Exposed split to Ia and Is by Fraction Symptomatic; Ia recovery after 4 days; Is split to direct recovery and hospital by Hospitalisation Fraction; hospital as a second-order 10-day delay; force of infection with Ia weighted at 50% of Is infectiousness.2023/24 Q3(b)
How should the 2025/26 quarantine-vaccination bubble plot be interpreted?x-axis is quarantine fraction, y-axis final vaccination fraction, and circle size is final attack rate. Largest circles should be at low quarantine and low vaccination; smallest at high/high. Explain that both policies reduce the susceptible-to-transmitting pathway, though the exact surface depends on model parameters.2025/26 Q2(c)

5. Supporting and Legacy-Backup Constructions

FrontBackEvidence
How is a resource-constrained vaccination flow formulated?Capacity=Number of Medics x Average Productivity; Vaccines Dispensed=MIN(Unvaccinated, Available Vaccines, Capacity). Move that flow from Unvaccinated to Vaccinated and subtract it from vaccine stock. The flow is zero if any binding resource is zero.2016/17, 2017/18, 2021/22
How is an effects-function relationship constructed safely?Normalise the independent variable by its reference, for example Normalised Ratio=(Students/Staff)/Reference Ratio. Use a dimensionless lookup multiplier, then Attrition Fraction=Reference Attrition Fraction x Effect(Normalised Ratio). Plot every stated anchor point and bounds.2016/17 Q4, 2018/19 Q4, 2019/20 Q3
State Little's Law and its condition.Average Stock = Average Throughput x Average Delay, equivalently Average Delay=Average Stock/Average Throughput. It applies to compatible averages in a stable system, where average inflow and outflow balance; it does not say each item has that exact delay.2017/18 Q4, 2018/19 Q1(c)
Convert dS/dt=t with S(0)=0 into a stock-flow and analytical answer.Use S=INTEG(t,0), with t as the simulation-time auxiliary. Analytically, S(t)=t^2/2, so S(20)=200. If Euler is requested instead, use the stated DT rather than the analytical solution.2017/18 Q2(a)
Convert logistic growth with decline into stock-flow form.For dP/dt=rP(1-P/C)-sP, use P=INTEG(Births-Decline,P0), Births=rP(1-P/C), Decline=sP. The carrying-capacity term is balancing; P reaches C only if s=0 and the system settles there.2021/22 Q2(a)
How is customer doubling estimated from net fractional growth?With fractional gain g1 and loss g2, net growth is g=g1-g2. For continuous exponential growth, C(t)=C0 e^(g t) and doubling time is ln(2)/g. State the model assumes a constant net fractional rate.2021/22 Q2(c)
How do you infer reinforcing versus balancing one-stock structure from a graph?Increasing at an increasing rate suggests reinforcing fractional growth, such as Inflow=kS. Rise towards a horizontal limit suggests balancing feedback, such as adjustment to a goal or carrying capacity. Give a separate stock-flow structure for each graph.2023/24 Q1(a)
What is an inflection point on a stock trajectory?It is where the stock curve changes concavity, from increasing slope to decreasing slope or vice versa. It corresponds to a local maximum or minimum of net flow, not automatically a maximum or minimum of stock.2017/18 Q2(c)
What is a control flag in a policy model?A parameter constrained to 0 or 1 that activates or deactivates a policy mechanism. For example, multiply a quarantine flow or quarantine fraction by the flag so 0 removes that pathway and 1 applies the stated policy.2024/25 Q3(b)
