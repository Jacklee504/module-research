Rapid exam refresher

Fractional flow=S times rate. Goal adjustment=(goal-current)/AT. AT sets speed, not final target. A first-order delay is a stock that smooths/retards input.

Adapted exam practice - Model a changing attrition fraction. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: separate fraction/flow. Quickest route: Outflow=S G; dG/dt=(Gstar-G)/AT. Answer: integrate G then use it in fractional decrease. Trap: add fraction directly to stock.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: interpret AT. Quickest route: small fast, large smooth/slow, sensitivity. Answer: AT controls transient speed, so choose from evidence and test it. Trap: changes final goal.
