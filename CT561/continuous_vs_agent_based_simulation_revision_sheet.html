Continuous versus Agent-Based Simulation

System dynamics models aggregate stocks/flows/feedback over continuous time; ABM models heterogeneous autonomous agents/local rules.

What the local papers show

Rapid exam refresher

System dynamics models aggregate stocks/flows/feedback over continuous time; ABM models heterogeneous autonomous agents/local rules. Choose aggregate feedback for totals and ABM for networks, discrete interaction/adaptation/emergence. Validate both structurally and behaviourally.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before application.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Syllabus-based: choose a commuter model. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: make abstraction choice. Quickest route: outputs -> heterogeneity/interactions -> model -> limitation. Answer: use stock-flow for aggregate demand/capacity; ABM for individual route choices/emergent queues; hybrid if necessary. Trap: ABM merely because detailed.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: validate behaviour. Quickest route: structure, calibration, holdout, sensitivity. Answer: verify rules/equations, calibrate, reproduce holdout peaks and test uncertainty. Trap: fit alone is validation.
