k-NN is supervised/lazy: vote/average k labelled neighbours.

Rapid exam refresher

k-NN is supervised/lazy: vote/average k labelled neighbours. k-means is unsupervised/eager: assign points to centroids then update means. Validate k for k-NN; use elbow/silhouette for k-means. Scale features; k-means favours roughly spherical numeric clusters.

Adapted exam practice - Compare k-NN and k-means. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: separate task/labels. Quickest route: labelled neighbours versus unlabelled centroids. Answer: k-NN predicts at query time; k-means forms clusters iteratively without labels. Trap: k-means predicts known class.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: choose scaling/k. Quickest route: z-score then inertia elbow/silhouette. Answer: scaling prevents large units dominating; select a diminishing-return k and verify usability. Trap: elbow automatic.
