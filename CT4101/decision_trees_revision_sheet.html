Rapid exam refresher

Entropy H(S)=-sum p log2p; IG=H(parent)-weighted child entropy. Recursively split on maximum gain then stop at purity/no features/small node. Pruning/depth/min-leaf reduce instability and overfit.

Adapted exam practice - Build a small classifier tree. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: calculate/select split. Quickest route: counts -> parent entropy -> weighted children -> subtract. Answer: choose maximum information gain because it reduces uncertainty most. Trap: omit child-size weights.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: give induction/stopping. Quickest route: base case -> best split -> recurse -> prune. Answer: return majority class at stopping cases; otherwise partition and recurse; validate pruning/depth. Trap: grow until memorisation.
