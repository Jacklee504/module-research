Rapid exam refresher

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before application.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Scope an ML project. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: map target to task. Quickest route: label, number, no labels; application/algorithm. Answer: spam classification, price regression and customer clustering have different targets/learners. Trap: clusters are ground-truth labels.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: explain dimensionality. Quickest route: sparse space -> unreliable neighbourhood -> select/reduce. Answer: use domain features, PCA/representations or regularisation and validate retained signal. Trap: drop features merely due to count.
