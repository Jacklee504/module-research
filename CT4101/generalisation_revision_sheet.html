Exam topic guide

Generalisation, Bias and Variance

High bias has high train/validation error; high variance has low train and high validation error.

What the local papers show

Over/underfitting and hypothesis-space questions appear in 2019/20 Q2(c), 2021/22 Q1(c), and 2023/24 Q3(a).

Rapid exam refresher

High bias has high train/validation error; high variance has low train and high validation error. A hypothesis language is the set of expressible functions. Diagnose with learning curves and held-out data; simplify, regularise, gather data or ensemble appropriately.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before application.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Diagnose a model error pattern. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: infer bias/variance. Quickest route: train-validation gap -> cause -> remedy. Answer: low train/high validation is high variance; regularise/prune/reduce capacity or gather representative data. Trap: call it high bias.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: choose representation with evidence. Quickest route: define model capacity and inspect learning curves. Answer: high similar errors imply underfit; wide gap overfit; choose by validation, not final-test tuning. Trap: repeatedly use test data.
