Rapid exam refresher

Fit preprocessing on training data only. Min-max x=(x-min)/(max-min); z-score=(x-mu)/sigma. Euclidean suits scaled continuous features; Hamming/matching suits discrete attributes. Avoid leakage and arbitrary numeric category codes.

Adapted exam practice - Prepare mixed data for k-NN. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: match metric to type. Quickest route: Euclidean numeric, Hamming categorical, safe encoding. Answer: use Euclidean on scaled measurements and mismatch distance on categories after appropriate encoding. Trap: Euclidean over colour codes.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: formula plus leakage control. Quickest route: train min/max or mean/sd, apply unchanged. Answer: min-max maps training range to [0,1]; z-score centres/scales by training statistics within every CV fold. Trap: test-set statistics.
