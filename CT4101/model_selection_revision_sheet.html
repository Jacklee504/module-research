Rapid exam refresher

Hyperparameters are chosen, not fitted weights: k, depth, C, learning rate. K-fold trains k models per repetition; stratification retains class proportions. Keep final test separate and fit preprocessing inside folds.

  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Select a classifier configuration. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: count folds/models. Quickest route: train four/test one, rotate, repeat. Answer: 5x5 CV fits 25 models/configuration and averages metrics/variation with class proportions retained. Trap: train on held-out fold.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: isolate selection/test. Quickest route: grid -> CV -> select/refit -> one test. Answer: choose plausible values from CV then refit on training before a single test report. Trap: test-based selection.
