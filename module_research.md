### Mandatory Workload Notes

- CT413 is the biggest CA opportunity in the year: 10 credits, 100% CA. Plan it as a full-year workload, roughly October to March.
- CT4104 is also strategically important because it is required and has 40% CA, but it should be treated as less exam-predictable because no local past papers are available.
- CT417 and CT4101 are required but more exam-heavy at 70% exam / 30% CA. CT4101 is highly predictable; CT417 is medium-high overall and high for the recent paper style.
- Official status caveat: CT413 should be confirmed with registration/programme guidance because the public GY350 page labels it optional while the CS project path likely makes it practically necessary.

## How to Use This Log

- Use this file to compare modules quickly.
- Prioritise modules with high CA weighting when there is time to prepare before deadlines.
- Use each module's detailed file for evidence, repeated exam patterns, and study strategy.
- Each module `details.md` starts with front matter for repeated facts such as status, semester, credits, assessment split, predictability, lecturer risk and the official module URL. The generated individual HTML pages use that front matter for their top summary.
- In exam-paper lecturer notes, a starred internal examiner is treated as the module lecturer for that paper. Unstarred internal examiners and public-page teacher/administrator lists are weighted less heavily when judging lecturer continuity.
