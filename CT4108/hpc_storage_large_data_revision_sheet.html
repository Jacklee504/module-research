CT4108 Revision

HPC, Storage and Large Data

Match a large-data workload to parallel compute, storage placement and movement constraints.

Rapid exam refresher

  • Amdahl: S=1/(serial+parallel/p); serial setup caps speedup.
  • Data locality, network/storage bandwidth and metadata can dominate CPU.
  • Partition/balance by access; avoid tiny-file and hot-key skew.
  • Replication versus erasure coding trades capacity, read and repair cost.

Paper evidence and scope

No CT4108 papers: syllabus-based.

Medium practice — 15 marks

10% serial, 90% parallel analysis. (a) [5] max speedup. (b) [5] I/O bottleneck. (c) [5] layout.

Answers

(a): Smax=1/.1=10. (b): one reader/metadata server serialises ingestion. (c): large balanced partitioned chunks near workers. Trap: claiming unlimited nodes scale.

Hard practice — 15 marks

Key skew causes stragglers. (a) [5] diagnose. (b) [5] mitigate. (c) [5] durability.

Answers

(a): compare partition size/runtime. (b): salt/split hot keys, repartition, use safe speculation. (c): choose replication for availability or erasure coding for capacity. Trap: average utilisation hides skew.
