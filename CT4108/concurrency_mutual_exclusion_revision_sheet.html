CT4108 Revision

Concurrency and Mutual Exclusion

Protect shared resources while explaining correctness and progress, not just naming a lock.

Rapid exam refresher

  • Mutual exclusion requires safety, progress and fairness; races are non-atomic read-modify-write.
  • Deadlock needs exclusive resources, hold-and-wait, no pre-emption and circular wait.
  • Distributed leases need fencing tokens: a paused old owner may act after expiry.

Paper evidence and scope

No CT4108 papers: syllabus-based.

Medium practice — 15 marks

Workers decrement stock. (a) [5] race. (b) [5] atomic fix. (c) [5] two-warehouse deadlock.

Answers

(a) Route/answer/trap: show both read old value then both write; sale count is wrong. Trap: calling a variable ‘thread-safe’.

(b): conditional atomic update/CAS decrements only if count>0; invariant: never negative, one decrement per sale. Trap: lock only read.

(c): global warehouse-ID acquisition order or atomic multi-resource transaction prevents circular wait. Trap: sleep/retry as proof.

Hard practice — 15 marks

Lease holder pauses 30 seconds. (a) [5] risk. (b) [5] fencing. (c) [5] recovery.

Answers

(a): stale owner writes after lease expiry. (b): monotonically increasing token; storage rejects token ≤ last seen. (c): idempotent operations, fresh lease and logged ownership change. Trap: expiry stops a paused process.
