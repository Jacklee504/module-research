Adapted exam practice - Rank pages for a query. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: read directed links. Quickest route: incoming source/outdegree -> sum -> damping -> iterate. Answer: add base teleport score and incoming divided scores. It measures endorsement, not necessarily query relevance. Trap: send full score along every edge.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: distinguish scope/scores. Quickest route: HITS two mutual scores versus global one. Answer: HITS computes query-focused hub/authority; PageRank one global damped score. HITS may drift/spam and costs per query. Trap: swap directions.
