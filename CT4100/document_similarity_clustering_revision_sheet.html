Adapted exam practice - Design article-search clusters. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: make documents comparable. Quickest route: title/abstract/keyword weights -> TF-IDF -> L2 normalise -> cosine. Answer: weight title/keywords above body and score articles by cosine of their normalised TF-IDF vectors. This controls length; citations/authors may be added only as scaled features. Trap: raw counts make long papers look similar.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: make clusters useful. Quickest route: candidate k -> repeated starts -> assign/update -> silhouette -> labels. Answer: run k-means over vectors for candidate k values, compare silhouette and inspect top terms; use clusters for browse facets/diversification. Hierarchical clustering helps when k is unknown. Trap: a high internal score proves user value.
