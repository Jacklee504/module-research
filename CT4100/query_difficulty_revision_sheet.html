Rapid exam refresher

Pre-retrieval: length, ambiguity, IDF/scope. Collection: document frequency/co-occurrence. Post-retrieval: score dispersion/top-result coherence. Predict before judgements to choose clarification, expansion or diversification.

Answer route

  1. Identify the calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation demanded.
  2. State the equation, algorithm or diagram before applying it.
  3. Finish with a topic-specific assumption, limitation or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Predict whether a help query is difficult. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: distinguish evidence sources. Quickest route: query-only, collection-wide, returned-list feature. Answer: short ambiguous wording is risky; low mean IDF/high df signals broad terms; dispersed scores/heterogeneous results suggest uncertainty. Trap: call a ranked-list feature pre-retrieval.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: use prediction as a decision. Quickest route: threshold -> clarification/diverse terms -> validate. Answer: prompt predicted-hard users with facets or diverse candidates and validate against effectiveness. Rare queries may be misspelled; coherent results can be wrong. Trap: call a proxy a judgement.
