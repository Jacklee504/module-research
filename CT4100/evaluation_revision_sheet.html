Retrieval evaluation

Precision=TP/(TP+FP), recall=TP/(TP+FN).

What the local papers show

Precision-recall construction is 2021/22 Q2(b) and 2024/25 Q3(b); 2023/24 Q3(a) and 2025/26 Q1(h) add user experience and MAP.

Rapid exam refresher

Precision=TP/(TP+FP), recall=TP/(TP+FN). At relevant ranks plot recall against precision. AP averages precision at relevant ranks; MAP averages AP over queries. Diversity/task-time complement judged relevance.

Adapted exam practice - Evaluate a ranked result list. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: show rank-by-rank calculation. Quickest route: scan ranks, update relevant/retrieved, record P@k/R@k. Answer: use all judged relevant documents as recall denominator and plot the points at relevant ranks; it exposes early versus late quality. Trap: use returned relevant items as recall denominator.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: aggregate fairly. Quickest route: AP per query -> mean -> diversity/time complement. Answer: AP is mean P@k at relevant ranks and MAP is mean AP. Pair it with diversity@k or task time; MAP needs judgements and can hide failures. Trap: average precision at every rank.
