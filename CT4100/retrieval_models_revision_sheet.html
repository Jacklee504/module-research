Rapid exam refresher

Boolean is exact but brittle; extended Boolean grades AND/OR. Vector space ranks cosine(q,d)=q dot d/(norm q norm d), normally TF-IDF. Inverted indexes map terms to postings. Term independence misses semantics.

Adapted exam practice - Compare two retrieval designs. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: connect index to ranking. Quickest route: postings -> TF-IDF -> dot/cosine. Answer: accumulate matched weighted postings and divide by vector norms; normalisation stops duplicated documents winning by raw count. Trap: scan every document per query.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: compare exact/graded matching. Quickest route: AND/OR failure -> p-norm effect -> limit. Answer: extended Boolean ranks partial weighted matches, emphasising weak terms for AND and strong alternatives for OR. Trap: call it cosine.
