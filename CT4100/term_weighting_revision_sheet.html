Term weighting

TF-IDF=tf log(N/df); cosine controls length.

What the local papers show

Axioms/weighting are 2021/22 Q3 and 2024/25 Q1(c); normalisation is 2024/25 Q1(a,b), BM25 is 2025/26 Q1(d,e).

Rapid exam refresher

TF-IDF=tf log(N/df); cosine controls length. Good weights grow with tf but saturate, discount common terms and manage length. BM25 uses IDF, saturated tf parameter k1 and length parameter b.

Adapted exam practice - Choose a document weighting scheme. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: relate formula to behaviour. Quickest route: tf, IDF, cosine, length. Answer: tf rewards evidence, IDF discounts common terms and cosine normalises length; duplication otherwise inflates score without relevance. Trap: say IDF rewards stop words.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: explain BM25 parameters. Quickest route: IDF times saturated tf times length correction. Answer: k1 controls saturation and b length normalisation; tune on held-out queries. Trap: treat parameters as labels.
