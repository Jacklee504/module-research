Rapid exam refresher

Rocchio updates q'=alpha q+beta mean(relevant)-gamma mean(nonrelevant). Pseudo-feedback assumes top k relevant. Local expansion extracts returned-set terms; global expansion uses ontology/thesaurus. Control drift and diversify candidates.

Adapted exam practice - Improve a sparse query. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: transform a vector with assumptions. Quickest route: original + relevant centroid - nonrelevant centroid. Answer: weight judged relevant documents positively and nonrelevant negatively, retain discriminative terms and allow graded feedback. It assumes relevant documents cluster. Trap: add every frequent term.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: contrast evidence/failure. Quickest route: local top-k versus fixed knowledge. Answer: pseudo-feedback adapts vocabulary without effort but drifts on bad initial results; ontology is stable but may be broad/outdated. Trap: call pseudo-feedback explicit feedback.
