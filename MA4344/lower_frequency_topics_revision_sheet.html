Practice question -- subgroup product (12 marks)

Inspired by: 2021/22 Question 2(a).

(a) If H≤G and N&lhd;G, prove HN is a subgroup. [6 marks]

Answer route: one-step subgroup test. Answer: HN is nonempty. For h1n1,h2n2∈HN, their quotient is h1n1n2-1h2-1=h1h2-1(h2n1n2-1h2-1), in HN because N is normal. Trap: commute n past h without normality.

(b) State the Second Isomorphism Theorem. [6 marks]

Answer: if N&lhd;G and H≤G, then HN/N≅H/(H∩N). Trap: omit the normality hypothesis.
