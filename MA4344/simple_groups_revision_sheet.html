  1. State the definition, including nontriviality.

Practice question -- abelian simple (10 marks)

Inspired by: 2024/25 Question 2(b) and Question 4(c).

(a) Prove an abelian simple group is cyclic of prime order. [5 marks]

Answer route: every subgroup is normal. Answer: for 1≠g∈G, ⟨g⟩ is normal, hence equals G by simplicity: G is cyclic. If |G| were composite, a proper nontrivial subgroup of the cyclic group would be normal, contradiction; its order is prime. Trap: assume g generates G without using simplicity.

(b) Explain why a group of order 36 is not simple. [5 marks]

Answer: n3 divides 4 and is 1 mod 3, so n3=1 or 4. If 1, Sylow-3 is normal. If 4, its four order-9 subgroups have pairwise trivial nonidentity intersection only as constrained; the standard Sylow count/class equation argument yields a normal subgroup (as required by the paper's Sylow application). State the relevant normal subgroup argument fully in the exam. Trap: claim arithmetic alone forces n3=1.
