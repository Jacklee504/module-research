Practice question -- groups of order 40 (14 marks)

Inspired by: 2022/23 Question 3(b).

(a) State the fundamental theorem of finite abelian groups. [3 marks]

Answer: every finite abelian group is isomorphic to a finite direct product of cyclic groups of prime-power order, uniquely up to order/rearrangement of factors. Trap: say every finite group is cyclic products.

(b) Classify abelian groups of order 40. [7 marks]

Answer route: 23 has partitions 3, 2+1, 1+1+1; multiply by C5. Thus C40, C20×C2, and C10×C2×C2. Trap: list C8×C5 separately from C40.

(c) State Cauchy's theorem. [4 marks]

Answer: if p is prime dividing the order of a finite group G, then G contains an element of order p. Trap: claim a subgroup of every divisor order.
