Cosets and Normal Subgroups

Build the correct bijection, then use index to establish normality.

What repeatedly appears in the papers

How to answer

  1. Define a map between coset sets and check it is well-defined.
  2. For index two, compare the unique non-H left and right cosets.
  3. Do not treat all subgroups as normal.

Practice question -- index two (12 marks)

Inspired by: 2023/24 and 2024/25 Question 2(a).

(a) Prove numbers of left and right cosets are equal. [5 marks]

Demand / quickest route: inverse gives a bijection. Answer: map gH to Hg-1. If gH=g'H then g'-1g∈H, hence g-1H=g'-1H; inverse construction is the same, so this is a bijection. Trap: map gH to Hg without checking it is well-defined.

(b) If [G:H]=2, prove H is normal. [7 marks]

Demand / quickest route: separate g∈H and g∉H. Answer: for g∈H, gH=H=Hg. Otherwise gH is the unique left coset distinct from H and Hg is the unique right coset distinct from H; both equal G\H, so gH=Hg. Thus H is normal. Trap: infer normality merely from finite index.
