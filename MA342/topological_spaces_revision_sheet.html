Rapid refresher

Core ruleExam distinction
A topology tau on X contains empty and X, is closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections. Cofinite: empty or complement finite; discrete: every subset open.A base covers X and each overlap refines around every point. Lower-limit base is [a,b).

Medium/hard adapted practice

Paper family: 2023/24 Q1 and 2024/25 Q1 open with topology definitions, finite examples, cofinite/discrete and lower-limit topology.

(a) On X={a,b}, decide whether {empty,X,{a}} is a topology and give a distinct topology. [8 marks]

Question demand: apply the definition/calculation with working. Quickest route: Check the three axioms; for a two-point set list all unions/intersections of listed opens.

Mark-aware answer: It is a topology: empty and X occur, every union remains one of the three sets, and finite intersections do too. The discrete topology P(X) is distinct.

Trap: Checking only pairwise unions is not a full explanation for a larger family.

(b) Show that the cofinite topology on an infinite set is a topology. [6 marks]

Question demand: Check empty/X, arbitrary unions, and finite intersections using finite complements. Quickest route: Check empty/X, arbitrary unions, and finite intersections using finite complements.

Mark-aware answer: Empty and X are cofinite. Any union of cofinite sets is cofinite because its complement is an intersection of finite sets (or the union may be X). A finite intersection has complement a finite union of finite sets. Thus all topology axioms hold.

Trap: Arbitrary intersections of cofinite sets need not be cofinite.

(c) Show [0,1) is both open and closed in the lower-limit topology. [4 marks]

Question demand: Use [a,b) basis sets and take complements. Quickest route: Use [a,b) basis sets and take complements.

Mark-aware answer: [0,1) is a basis element, so it is open. Its complement is (-infinity,0) union [1,infinity), each a union of lower-limit basis intervals, hence open; therefore [0,1) is closed.

Trap: Ordinary Euclidean intuition gives the wrong answer here.
