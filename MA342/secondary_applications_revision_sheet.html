(a) A triangulation has V=10 and every edge is in two triangular faces. Find E,F on the sphere. [6 marks]

Question demand: apply the definition/calculation with working. Quickest route: Use 3F=2E and V-E+F=2.

Mark-aware answer: From 3F=2E, F=2E/3; 10-E+2E/3=2 gives E=24,F=16.

Trap: Do not use Euler's sphere formula for a torus or arbitrary surface.

(b) Use Euler's formula to derive the planar triangulation relation E=3V-6. [6 marks]

Question demand: For a connected planar triangulation use 3F=2E and V-E+F=2. Quickest route: For a connected planar triangulation use 3F=2E and V-E+F=2.

Mark-aware answer: Each triangular face has three edge-sides and each edge borders two faces, so 3F=2E. Substitution into V-E+F=2 gives V-E+2E/3=2, hence E=3V-6.

Trap: Boundary faces or non-triangulations need different incidence counts.

(c) State Brouwer's fixed-point theorem in a usable form. [4 marks]

Question demand: Name the domain and continuity hypotheses, then conclusion. Quickest route: Name the domain and continuity hypotheses, then conclusion.

Mark-aware answer: Every continuous map from a nonempty compact convex subset of Rn to itself has a fixed point. For a disc or interval, first verify the map stays in that same set.
