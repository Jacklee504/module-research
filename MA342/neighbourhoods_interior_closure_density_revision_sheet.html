Medium/hard adapted practice

Paper family: 2023/24 Q1/Q2 and 2024/25 Q1/Q2 explicitly test neighbourhood, interior, closure and density, including cofinite examples.

(a) Show an infinite A is dense in R with the cofinite topology. [8 marks]

Question demand: apply the definition/calculation with working. Quickest route: Take arbitrary nonempty open U; its complement is finite, so U cannot avoid infinite A.

Mark-aware answer: Every nonempty cofinite open U intersects infinite A, since otherwise A is contained in the finite complement of U. Hence every neighbourhood meets A and closure(A)=R.

Trap: This statement is false in the usual topology for arbitrary infinite A.

(b) Compute the interior and closure of (0,1] in the usual topology on R. [6 marks]

Question demand: Find the largest open subset and smallest closed superset. Quickest route: Find the largest open subset and smallest closed superset.

Mark-aware answer: The interior is (0,1), since 1 has no open interval contained in (0,1]. The closure is [0,1], since every neighbourhood of 0 meets the set and [0,1] is closed containing it.

Trap: Do not include 0 in the interior or omit 0 from the closure.

(c) State an equivalent neighbourhood test for x in closure(A). [4 marks]

Question demand: Give the iff statement and distinguish it from interior. Quickest route: Give the iff statement and distinguish it from interior.

Mark-aware answer: x belongs to closure(A) iff every open neighbourhood U of x satisfies U intersect A nonempty. In contrast, x is in interior(A) iff some open neighbourhood of x lies inside A.

Trap: One intersecting neighbourhood is not enough for closure.
