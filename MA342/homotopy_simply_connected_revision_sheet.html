Medium/hard adapted practice

Paper family: Homotopy is a Q4 core in 2023/24 and 2024/25; 2023/24 additionally asks simply connected/star-shaped arguments.

(a) Show any f,g:R^m to R^n are homotopic. [8 marks]

Question demand: apply the definition/calculation with working. Quickest route: Use linear interpolation H(x,t)=(1-t)f(x)+tg(x); closure of Rn under linear combinations gives a valid image.

Mark-aware answer: H is continuous as a sum/product of continuous maps, H(x,0)=f(x), and H(x,1)=g(x). Hence f and g are homotopic.

Trap: This formula may leave Y when Y is a non-convex subspace.

(b) Show a star-shaped subset S of Rn is contractible. [6 marks]

Question demand: Choose a star centre x0 and use straight-line contraction. Quickest route: Choose a star centre x0 and use straight-line contraction.

Mark-aware answer: For x0 in S define H(x,t)=(1-t)x+t x0. Star-shapedness puts H(x,t) in S; H is continuous, H(x,0)=x and H(x,1)=x0. Thus idS is homotopic to a constant map.

Trap: The line segment must remain in S; this fails for an annulus.

(c) Distinguish path connected from simply connected. [4 marks]

Question demand: State the additional loop condition and give a standard counterexample. Quickest route: State the additional loop condition and give a standard counterexample.

Mark-aware answer: Path connected means any two points are joined by a path. Simply connected additionally requires every loop to be homotopic to a constant loop. S1 is path connected but not simply connected.

Trap: Connected alone is weaker than path connected.
