MA342 Topic Guide

Subspaces and Constructions

Build and use subspace, product and quotient topologies.

Purpose

Translate a construction into its defining open sets and then prove the requested property from that definition.Exam use: Construction, defining preimage/basis rule, ambient versus subspace openness and saturation are visible.

Question plan

  1. Name the construction and its underlying set.
  2. Write the defining rule for opens or a basis.
  3. Use the universal map only when its continuity criterion is relevant.
  4. Question interpretation: For subspace, write U=X intersect O; for product, use basis rectangles; for quotient, test openness through the quotient map preimage.

Definitions

For Y⊆X, the subspace topology is {Y∩U:U open in X}. A basis for X×Y consists of U×V with U,V open. The quotient topology declares W open when q⁻¹(W) is open.Rapid recall: Subspace opens are X intersect ambient opens; product topology has basis UxV; quotient topology declares U open exactly when q^{-1}(U) is open.

Proof method

For subspaces, express the target set as Y∩U. For quotients, pull the set back along q; never assume images of open sets are open.Exam workflow: Identify the defining map or inclusion, translate the claim to preimages/basis elements, and keep saturation in mind for quotient sets.

Worked simple example

If X=R and Y=[0,1], then [0,1/2)=Y∩(-1,1/2), so it is open in the subspace Y.

Medium/hard paper-style synthesis

Let q:[0,1]->S^1 identify 0~1 via q(t)=e^{2 pi i t}. For an open arc U in S^1 avoiding 1, q^{-1}(U) is an open interval in (0,1), hence open in [0,1]. For an arc containing 1, its preimage is [0,epsilon) union (1-epsilon,1], also subspace-open. Thus q is compatible with the quotient topology; the endpoint pieces must both be included.

Graduated exam bridges

1. Foundation: List the subspace opens on {0} inside R. Check: ∅ and {0}.

2. Exam style: Let q:[0,1]→S¹ send t to e²πit. Explain why q identifies 0 and 1 and supports the quotient description of S¹. Check: state the equivalence relation and use q⁻¹ for openness.

Hard paper-style synthesis

Paper-style synthesis: prove projection pi_X:XxY->X is open. Check a basic open rectangle UxV maps to U when V nonempty; an arbitrary open set is a union of basic rectangles, and projection commutes with unions, so its image is open.

Mistakes

  • Using U∩Y but forgetting U must be open in X.
  • Claiming every subset of a product is a basis element.
  • Replacing quotient preimages by images.
  • Recovery rule: The image of an open set under a quotient map need not be open unless its full saturated preimage is open.
