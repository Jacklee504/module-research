Rapid refresher

Core ruleExam distinction
Shift: C=P+k mod 27; affine: C=aP+b with gcd(a,27)=1; Hill: C=AP and P=A-1C; Vigenere adds a repeated key.A shift uses one displacement; Hill uses invertible matrices; Kerckhoffs says the system may be public but the key secret.

Medium/hard adapted practice

Paper family: 2018/19, 2021/22, 2022/23 and 2023/24 Q1 repeat a shift decode, a Hill/affine calculation and a short principle or Vigenere part.

(a) Known E maps to S in Z27. Decode the first word of a ciphertext and explain why a non-unit Hill determinant is fatal. [5 marks]

Question demand: apply the definition/calculation with working. Quickest route: Find k=S-E mod 27, then subtract it from every symbol; for Hill check det(A) is a unit before inverting.

Mark-aware answer: k=14 (with A=0,...,Z=25, space=26). Thus P=C-14 mod 27; write the converted first word. For Hill, det(A) must have gcd(det(A),27)=1 so A-1 exists and decryption is unique.

Trap: Do not use mod 26 when the paper gives the 27-character alphabet.

(b) Given A=[[2,3],[7,5]] over Z26, find A-1. [6 marks]

Question demand: Compute det=-11=15 mod 26; invert 15 mod 26, then multiply the adjugate. Quickest route: Compute det=-11=15 mod 26; invert 15 mod 26, then multiply the adjugate.

Mark-aware answer: 15-1=7 mod 26. Thus A-1=7[[5,-3],[-7,2]]=[[9,5],[3,14]] mod 26. Check AA-1=I mod 26.

Trap: Reducing negative entries is part of the calculation; do not use ordinary real inversion.

(c) Use the key FAB to encrypt BEATLES in the 27-symbol alphabet. [4 marks]

Question demand: Repeat key FABFABF, convert symbols to numbers, and add componentwise mod 27. Quickest route: Repeat key FABFABF, convert symbols to numbers, and add componentwise mod 27.

Mark-aware answer: With A=0, F=5, B=1, the key values are 5,0,1,5,0,1,5. Add these to B,E,A,T,L,E,S modulo 27 and convert each resulting residue back using the stated table.

Trap: A Vigenere key is repeated; it is not a single Caesar displacement.
