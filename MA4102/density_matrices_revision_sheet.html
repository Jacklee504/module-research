Density Matrices, Purity and Entropy

Turn states or ensembles into ρ, then use trace identities rather than intuition.

Purpose

Density matrices are a highly repeated bridge between state, mixedness and entanglement questions.Exam use: Outer products, weights, matrix validity, purity/eigenvalues and pure-versus-mixed conclusion are all present.

Question plan

  1. For pure |ψ⟩, form ρ=|ψ⟩⟨ψ|.
  2. For an ensemble, form ρ=∑kpk|ψk⟩⟨ψk|.
  3. Check Hermitian, positive and Tr(ρ)=1.
  4. Compute Tr(ρ2) or eigenvalues for entropy.
  5. Question interpretation: For construct, form weighted outer products; for pure/mixed, calculate Tr(rho^2); for entropy, find eigenvalues before applying -sum lambda log lambda.

Notation and derivation

Purity is P=Tr(ρ2): P=1 for a pure state and P<1 for a nontrivial mixture. S(ρ)=−Tr(ρlog2ρ)=−∑λjlog2λj, using eigenvalues λj.Rapid recall: rho is positive semidefinite with trace 1; pure iff rho^2=rho iff Tr(rho^2)=1; a proper mixture and a coherent superposition can have the same basis probabilities but different off-diagonal entries.Exam workflow: Write each ket as a column, form outer products, add with probabilities, then check Hermiticity, trace and eigenvalues.

Worked calculation

For |+⟩=(|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2, ρ=½[[1,1],[1,1]]. Since ρ2=ρ, P=1 and S=0.

Practice 1 — a nonorthogonal two-state ensemble [9 marks]

Prompt. Build ρ for the equally weighted ensemble {|0>,|+>}, find its eigenvalues and purity, and classify it. Question-demand diagnosis. The ensemble weights must be applied to outer products before any purity test. Fastest route. Add the two weighted matrices, use trace and determinant for the 2×2 eigenvalues, then evaluate Tr(ρ²). Topic-specific trap. Equal classical weights do not imply the maximally mixed state when the preparation kets are nonorthogonal.

The ensemble {1/2,|0>; 1/2,|+>} has rho=(1/2)[[1,0],[0,0]]+(1/4)[[1,1],[1,1]]=[[3/4,1/4],[1/4,1/4]]. Its determinant is 1/8 and trace 1, so eigenvalues are (1 +/- 1/sqrt(2))/2. Purity Tr(rho^2)=1-2 det rho=3/4<1, hence mixed.

Graduated exam bridge

1. Find ρ and purity for ½|0⟩⟨0|+½|1⟩⟨1|. Check: diag(½,½), P=½.

2. Given a 2×2 density matrix, find its eigenvalues and Von Neumann entropy. Check: entropy uses eigenvalues, not entries.

Practice 2 — distinguish coherence from maximal mixing [8 marks]

Prompt. Compare the constructed ensemble state with I/2 using entries, purity and Bloch-vector interpretation. Question-demand diagnosis. A comparison needs matched quantities, not just the word “mixed”. Fastest route. Read the off-diagonal entries, then contrast Tr(ρ²) and the Bloch vector. Topic-specific trap. Identical-looking diagonal populations do not determine whether a state has coherence.

Paper-style synthesis: compare this with the maximally mixed state I/2. Check both may give nontrivial basis probabilities, but I/2 has purity 1/2 and zero Bloch vector, while the ensemble state has coherence 1/4 and purity 3/4.

Mistakes

  • Writing |ψ⟩⟨ψ| without conjugating the bra.
  • Calling every trace-one matrix a state.
  • Using Tr(ρ) as purity.
  • Recovery rule: A convex decomposition is not unique; do not infer a physical preparation uniquely from rho.
