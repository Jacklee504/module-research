Build ρ, trace out the right subsystem, then interpret purity and entropy.

How to answer

  1. Fix the basis order |00⟩,|01⟩,|10⟩,|11⟩.
  2. Outer-product the vector with its conjugate transpose.
  3. For a partial trace, retain terms with equal traced indices.
  4. Diagonalise only the reduced matrix before evaluating entropy.

Practice question -- reduced state (17 marks)

Inspired by: 2021/22 and 2024/25 Question 2.

Let |ψ⟩=(|00⟩+|11⟩)/√2.

(a) Calculate ρAB and ρB. [7 marks]

Demand / quickest route: take one outer product, then trace A. Answer: ρAB=½(|00⟩⟨00|+|00⟩⟨11|+|11⟩⟨00|+|11⟩⟨11|). TrA removes cross terms because ⟨0|1⟩=0, giving ρB=I/2. Trap: retaining |0⟩⟨1| after tracing the unequal A labels.

(b) Find purity and von Neumann entropy of ρB. [5 marks]

Demand / quickest route: use eigenvalues 1/2,1/2. Answer: Tr((I/2)2)=1/2 and S=-(2)(1/2 log21/2)=1 bit. Trap: calling the reduced state pure because the joint state is pure.

(c) State what this says about entanglement. [5 marks]

Demand / quickest route: connect mixed reduction with a pure bipartite state. Answer: entropy one (nonzero) means the pure joint state is entangled; indeed it is maximally entangled for two qubits. Trap: infer separability from the diagonal reduced matrix.
