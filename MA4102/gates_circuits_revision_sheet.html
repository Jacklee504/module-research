MA4102 Topic Guide

Gates and Circuits

Track basis states wire-by-wire and prove gate identities as matrices.

What repeatedly appears in the papers

Gate identities and circuit outputs occur in 2022/23 Question 1(b), 2025/26 Question 1(c,d), while 2024/25 begins with related unitary/density calculations. Expect 6--9 mark truth-table or matrix reasoning.

How to answer

  1. Fix wire order and process gates left-to-right.
  2. For an equality, verify on a computational basis or multiply matrices.
  3. Keep tensor identities explicit when a one-qubit gate acts in a larger space.

Practice question -- controlled gates (18 marks)

Inspired by: 2022/23 Question 1(b) and 2025/26 Question 1(c,d).

(a) Give the truth table of controlled-H. [6 marks]

Demand / quickest route: split on the control. Answer: |00⟩↦|00⟩, |01⟩↦|01⟩, |10⟩↦|1⟩(|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2, |11⟩↦|1⟩(|0⟩-|1⟩)/√2. Trap: apply H to the control wire.

(b) Prove (H⊗H)CNOT1,2(H⊗H)=CNOT2,1. [9 marks]

Demand / quickest route: verify the two linear maps agree on |00⟩,|01⟩,|10⟩,|11⟩. Answer: applying the left side gives respectively |00⟩,|11⟩,|10⟩,|01⟩, exactly the action |a,b⟩↦|a⊕b,b⟩ of reversed CNOT. Linearity proves equality. Trap: compare only one input state.

(c) State one audit check. [3 marks]

Answer: every gate and their product must be unitary, so outputs of an orthonormal basis remain orthonormal. Trap: use a probability check in place of the requested operator identity.
