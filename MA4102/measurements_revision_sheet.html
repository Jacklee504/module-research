Practice question -- parity measurement (20 marks)

Inspired by: 2025/26 Question 5(c) and 2024/25 Question 3(ii).

Let Πe=|00⟩⟨00|+|11⟩⟨11| and Πo=|01⟩⟨01|+|10⟩⟨10|.

(a) Show these form a projective measurement. [8 marks]

Demand / quickest route: use orthogonal basis projectors. Answer: each is Hermitian and idempotent; cross terms vanish, and Πe+Πo=I on the four-dimensional computational basis. Thus they satisfy the projective-measurement requirements. Trap: show only completeness.

(b) Measure |β00⟩=(|00⟩+|11⟩)/√2. [6 marks]

Demand / quickest route: apply Πe. Answer: Πe|β00⟩=|β00⟩, so p(even)=1 and the post-state is unchanged; p(odd)=0. Trap: add the two amplitudes then square.

(c) Give p(even) for (|00⟩+|01⟩+|10⟩)/√3. [6 marks]

Answer: projection leaves |00⟩/√3, hence p(even)=1/3 and conditional state |00⟩. Trap: treat parity as a rank-one measurement.
