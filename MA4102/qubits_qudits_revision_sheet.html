How to answer

  1. Write the stated basis and the amplitude for each requested outcome.
  2. Check the norm before applying Born's rule.
  3. Square the modulus, never the complex amplitude itself.
  4. State the collapsed vector, not merely its probability.

Practice question -- qutrit state (17 marks)

Inspired by: 2022/23 Question 2(a,b) and 2025/26 Question 2(a,b).

Let |ψ⟩=(|0⟩+2i|1⟩+3i|2⟩)/√14.

(a) Find p(2) and the state after outcome 2. [7 marks]

Demand / quickest route: read the |2⟩ amplitude and apply Born's rule. Answer: ⟨2|ψ⟩=3i/√14, so p(2)=|3i/√14|2=9/14. A rank-one computational measurement collapses the system to |2⟩. Trap: writing (3i/√14)2=-9/14.

(b) Write ρ=|ψ⟩⟨ψ|. [4 marks]

Demand / quickest route: form column times conjugate transpose. Answer: ρ=(1/14)[[1,-2i,-3i],[2i,4,6],[3i,6,9]]. It is Hermitian and has trace one. Trap: transpose without complex conjugation.

(c) Give all computational-basis probabilities. [6 marks]

Demand / quickest route: square each coefficient modulus. Answer: (1/14,4/14,9/14), summing to one; this is the required normalisation audit. Trap: omit the sum-to-one check.
