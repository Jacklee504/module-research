Practice question -- QFT (20 marks)

Inspired by: 2024/25 Question 4(ii) and 2025/26 Question 3(b).

(a) Define QFT3 and calculate QFT3|0⟩. [8 marks]

Demand / quickest route: substitute j=0. Answer: F3|j⟩=1/√3∑k=02ωjk|k⟩, ω=e2πi/3; hence F3|0⟩=(|0⟩+|1⟩+|2⟩)/√3. Trap: use a qubit Hadamard formula.

(b) Calculate F3(|0⟩+ω|1⟩+ω2|2⟩)/√3. [8 marks]

Demand / quickest route: recognise this as F3|1⟩. Answer: F32|1⟩=|-1 mod 3⟩=|2⟩ under this convention. A direct root-of-unity sum gives the same result. Trap: forget the sign/convention check for F2.

(c) Give one normalisation check. [4 marks]

Answer: F is unitary, so the norm remains one; the three output probabilities sum to one. Trap: claim each amplitude has modulus one.
