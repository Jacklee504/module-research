No-Cloning and Core Proofs

State the assumed universal operation, apply it to two arbitrary states, and force a contradiction.

Purpose

No-cloning is a repeated proof request; its proof structure is concise and high-return.Exam use: Universal assumption, fixed blank, preserved quantity/linearity, explicit unequal states and qualified conclusion are present.

Question plan

  1. Assume a unitary U clones every |ψ⟩ with blank |b⟩.
  2. Write U(|ψ⟩|b⟩)=|ψ⟩|ψ⟩ and similarly for |φ⟩.
  3. Take inner products and use unitary preservation.
  4. Choose nonorthogonal distinct states to contradict the result.
  5. Question interpretation: For prove impossible, assume one fixed physical operation works on arbitrary inputs, apply it to two states, and compare inner products or linear superpositions.

Notation and derivation

Unitarity gives ⟨φ|ψ⟩=⟨φ|ψ⟩2. Let c=⟨φ|ψ⟩. Then c=c2, so c is 0 or 1; arbitrary distinct nonorthogonal states have neither value. Thus no universal cloner exists.Rapid recall: A universal cloner would satisfy U|psi>|0>=|psi>|psi>; unitarity preserves inner products, forcing <phi|psi>=<phi|psi>^2, possible only for orthogonal or identical pairs.Exam workflow: Quantify over arbitrary states, keep the blank state fixed, take inner products on both sides, solve the resulting scalar equation, and state the contradiction.

Worked calculation

Take |ψ⟩=|0⟩ and |φ⟩=|+⟩. Their overlap is 1/√2. Cloning would require 1/√2=1/2, impossible.

Medium/hard paper-style synthesis

Assume U clones |0> and |+>. Input overlap is <0|+><0|0>=1/sqrt2. Output overlap is (<0|+>)^2=1/2. Unitarity requires equality, but 1/sqrt2 !=1/2, contradiction. Thus no unitary clones this nonorthogonal pair, and hence no universal perfect cloner exists.

Graduated exam bridge

1. Fill in the inner-product line in the cloning proof. Check: the blank register contributes ⟨b|b⟩=1.

2. Prove unitaries preserve purity: Tr[(UρU†)2]=Tr(ρ2). Check: use U†U=I and cyclicity of trace.

Hard paper-style synthesis

Paper-style synthesis: use linearity instead. Check if U clones |0> and |1>, then on |+>|0> linearity gives (|00>+|11>)/sqrt2, whereas a clone of |+> is (|00>+|01>+|10>+|11>)/2; they differ.

Mistakes

  • Only showing two basis states can be cloned.
  • Forgetting the claim is universal.
  • Calling c=c2 a contradiction without selecting nonorthogonal states.
  • Recovery rule: The theorem forbids unknown arbitrary-state perfect cloning, not copying a known orthogonal classical basis.

Checklist

  • I can reproduce the four proof moves.
  • I name the unitary and blank state.
  • I can use trace cyclicity in companion proofs.
  • Final 60-second audit: Universal assumption, fixed blank, preserved quantity/linearity, explicit unequal states and qualified conclusion are present.
