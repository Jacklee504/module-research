Practice question -- Pauli channel (15 marks)

Inspired by: 2025/26 Question 4(d).

Let E0=√(1-3λ/4)I and E1,2,3=√(λ/4){X,Y,Z}.

(a) Verify trace preservation. [6 marks]

Demand / quickest route: Pauli matrices are unitary. Answer: ∑Ek†Ek=(1-3λ/4)I+3(λ/4)I=I. For 0≤λ≤1 all weights are nonnegative. Trap: sum Ek instead of Ek†Ek.

(b) Give Φ(ρ). [5 marks]

Answer: Φ(ρ)=(1-3λ/4)ρ+(λ/4)(XρX+YρY+ZρZ). Trap: omit the adjoint on the right in the Kraus rule.

(c) Describe the effect. [4 marks]

Answer: it randomly applies a Pauli error; increasing λ depolarises the qubit, shrinking its Bloch vector toward the maximally mixed state. Trap: describe it as a deterministic X flip.
