MA4102 Topic Guide

Quantum Teleportation

Follow the Bell measurement and attach the correction to Alice's classical bits.

What repeatedly appears in the papers

Drawing the teleportation circuit is explicit in 2024/25 Question 4(iii). Bell-state measurement, rotated bases, and Bob's conditional state recur in 2022/23 and 2025/26 Question 5(a,b). Treat it as an 8--9 mark circuit/state-tracking family.

Rapid refresher

How to answer

  1. Label the input, Alice resource, and Bob wires.
  2. Show both classical bits and their controlled Z/X corrections.
  3. For a state question, condition on Alice's named outcome before normalising Bob's state.

Practice question -- teleportation circuit (18 marks)

Inspired by: 2024/25 Question 4(iii) and 2025/26 Question 5(b).

(a) Specify a circuit teleporting |ψ⟩=α|0⟩+β|1⟩. [8 marks]

Demand / quickest route: give operations in time order. Answer: prepare/share |β00⟩ on Alice/Bob; CNOT from input to Alice's resource qubit; H on input; measure both Alice qubits producing a,b; send a,b; Bob applies Xb then Za. His final state is |ψ⟩. Trap: omit the classical communication/corrections.

(b) If outcomes are a=1,b=0, what was Bob's pre-correction state and correction? [5 marks]

Demand / quickest route: invert the correction rule. Answer: pre-correction state is Z|ψ⟩ (up to global phase); apply Z to recover |ψ⟩. Trap: swap the X and Z bit roles.

(c) Why is no-cloning not violated? [5 marks]

Answer: Alice's input is measured and no longer available as |ψ⟩; Bob recovers one state only after classical bits arrive. Entanglement plus two classical bits transmits the state without making two copies. Trap: say the Bell pair itself contains a hidden copy.
