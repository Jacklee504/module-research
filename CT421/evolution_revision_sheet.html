Rapid exam refresher

GA uses population, fitness, selection, crossover, mutation and replacement. Schema theorem favours short low-order above-average patterns; selection exploits, mutation explores, crossover recombines but can disrupt. Penalise or repair infeasible candidates.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the algorithm, equation or diagram before applying it.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Allocate jobs to constrained processors. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: encode/score legal decisions. Quickest route: gene per task -> decode -> objective minus penalty/repair. Answer: gene i selects its processor; reward low makespan/value while penalising/repairing capacity violations. Trap: ignore infeasibility.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: relate operators to balance. Quickest route: selection, crossover, mutation, diversity. Answer: tournament selection exploits, crossover combines valid segments, mutation changes assignments and restores exploration. Trap: crossover always improves fitness.
