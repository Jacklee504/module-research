Rapid exam refresher

BFS expands shallowest, DFS deepest, iterative deepening repeats depth-limited DFS. BFS/ID complete under finite branching; BFS memory high, DFS low but not generally complete. Minimax backs up max/min utilities; alpha-beta prunes when alpha>=beta.

Adapted exam practice - Trace game search. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: justify minimax values/prunes. Quickest route: leaves -> min/max backup -> alpha/beta bounds. Answer: evaluate leaves/heuristics, propagate alternating values and prune only at alpha>=beta. Trap: prune merely bad-looking leaf.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: compare resources/guarantees. Quickest route: frontier order, time, memory, completeness. Answer: BFS is complete/high-memory; DFS low-memory/incomplete risk; iterative deepening has BFS completeness with DFS memory. Trap: DFS optimal.
