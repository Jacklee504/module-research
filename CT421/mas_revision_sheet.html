Rapid exam refresher

English auction ascends; Dutch descends to first acceptance. Dominant strategy beats alternatives regardless of others; Nash has no unilateral improvement. Speech acts include propose/accept/reject/inform. Multi-attribute negotiation needs utilities beyond price.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the algorithm, equation or diagram before applying it.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Allocate a delivery contract. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: mechanics, strategy, limitation. Quickest route: price direction -> bidder decision -> caveat. Answer: English bidders remain to value; Dutch bidders balance waiting and losing. Private information, collusion and non-price quality can harm allocation. Trap: Dutch ascends.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: negotiate more than price. Quickest route: speech acts + weighted utility/Pareto offers. Answer: exchange offers/counteroffers over price, deadline and quality; Nash stability is not necessarily fair. Trap: one price captures all preferences.
