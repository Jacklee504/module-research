Rapid exam refresher

Explainability covers evidence, contestability and audit. Use feature attribution/saliency and counterfactuals with limits. Artificial life produces emergence from local rules. ACO samples routes using pheromone/heuristics, reinforces good paths and evaporates trails.

Answer route

  1. Identify calculation, representation, comparison, design or evaluation.
  2. State the algorithm, equation or diagram before applying it.
  3. Finish with a specific limitation, assumption or trade-off.

Adapted exam practice - Explain black-box triage and ACO. [15]

(a) [7-9 marks]

(a) Demand: link opacity to risk/two remedies. Quickest route: harm/audit -> attribution/counterfactual -> limit. Answer: opaque outputs complicate bias checks/accountability; use attribution and counterfactuals but validate stability/non-causality. Trap: heatmap proves causality.

(b) [7-8 marks]

(b) Demand: give iterative ACO mechanism. Quickest route: construct -> evaluate -> reinforce -> evaporate. Answer: ants prefer pheromone/short edges, short paths deposit more pheromone, evaporation preserves exploration. Trap: reinforce every path equally.
