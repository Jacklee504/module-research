Practice question -- continuity (14 marks)

Inspired by: 2018/19 Question 2 and 2022/23 Question 2(b).

(a) State measure and probability-space definitions. [6 marks]

Answer: a measure maps a sigma-algebra to [0,∞], sends ∅ to 0, and is countably additive on pairwise disjoint sets; a probability space is (Ω,&F,P) with P(Ω)=1. Trap: replace countable by finite additivity.

(b) Prove continuity from below for An↑A. [8 marks]

Route / answer: set B1=A1, Bn=An\An-1. The Bn are disjoint, A=disjoint union Bn, and μ(AN)=∑n≤Nμ(Bn)→μ(A). Trap: invoke additivity on nested An.
