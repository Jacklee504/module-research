Practice question -- xn (18 marks)

Inspired by: 2022/23 and 2023/24 Question 1(g).

(a) Find the pointwise limit of fn(x)=xn on [0,1]. [5 marks]

Answer: f(x)=0 for 0≤x<1 and f(1)=1. Trap: say zero everywhere.

(b) Prove convergence is not uniform. [6 marks]

Answer route: sup difference. Answer: for x<1, |xn-0| has supremum 1 (approach 1 from below), so sup|fn-f|=1 for every n. Trap: evaluate only at x=1, where the difference is zero.

(c) Prove almost uniform convergence. [7 marks]

Answer: for ε>0 remove E=(1-ε,1], of measure ε. On [0,1-ε], sup xn=(1-ε)n→0. Trap: remove {1} only; it does not give uniform convergence.
