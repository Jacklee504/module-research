Practice question -- choose the theorem (20 marks)

Inspired by: 2022/23 and 2023/24 Question 4(c).

(a) State MCT. [6 marks]

Answer: for nonnegative measurable fn increasing pointwise to f, ∫f dμ=lim ∫fndμ, possibly infinite. Trap: omit monotonicity/nonnegativity.

(b) If f,g≥0 are measurable, prove ∫(f+g)=∫f+∫g using MCT. [8 marks]

Route / answer: choose increasing simple fn↑f and gn↑g. Then fn+gn↑f+g and simple-function additivity gives the equality after MCT. Trap: assume additivity for arbitrary functions before the proof.

(c) Which theorem fits dominated nonmonotone fn? [6 marks]

Answer: DCT, provided one integrable g dominates |fn| and pointwise/a.e. convergence holds. Trap: invoke MCT just because fn≥0.
