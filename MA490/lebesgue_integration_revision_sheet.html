Practice question -- definitions and calculation (18 marks)

Inspired by: 2023/24 Question 4(a,b).

(a) Define the integral of a measurable simple function and of nonnegative f. [6 marks]

Answer: use the two formulas in the table, with ai≥0 for the nonnegative simple construction; the supremum ranges over measurable simple functions below f. Trap: write a Riemann limiting sum.

(b) Let s=2χA+3χB, A∩B=∅, μ(A)=4, μ(B)=1. Find ∫s. [5 marks]

Answer: 2(4)+3(1)=11. Trap: add values rather than value times measure.

(c) Explain integrability of f. [7 marks]

Answer: f is integrable exactly when ∫|f|dμ<∞; only then are positive and negative integrals both finite and their difference defined. Trap: allow ∞-∞.
