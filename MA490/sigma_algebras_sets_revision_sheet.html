Practice question -- set closure (15 marks)

Inspired by: 2019/20 Question 2 and 2021/22 Question 2.

(a) Define a sigma-algebra. [3 marks]

Answer: a collection A⊆P(X) containing X, closed under complements relative to X and countable unions. Trap: use arbitrary unions.

(b) Prove A\B∈A whenever A,B∈A. [4 marks]

Answer: Bc∈A and A\B=A∩Bc=(Ac∪B)c∈A. Trap: assume finite intersection closure without deriving it.

(c) Show Cn=An\⋃k<nAk are disjoint and union to ⋃An. [8 marks]

Answer: each point is assigned to its first membership index; this proves disjointness and equality, enabling measure subadditivity. Trap: call the original An disjoint.
