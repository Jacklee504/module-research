Practice question -- product closure (19 marks)

Inspired by: 2022/23 and 2023/24 Question 3(b).

(a) Define measurability of f:X→R. [3 marks]

Answer: f-1(B)∈A for every Borel B⊆R; equivalently {x:f(x)>a}∈A for all a. Trap: say f maps measurable points to measurable points.

(b) Prove f2 is measurable if f is. [8 marks]

Answer route: composition with continuous x↦x2, or split the threshold inverse image into f>√a and f<-√a. State the zero/negative cases too. Trap: use monotonicity of square on all R.

(c) Prove fg is measurable. [8 marks]

Answer: with measurable sums and squares, fg=((f+g)2-f2-g2)/2 is measurable. Trap: assume product closure, which is what must be proved.
