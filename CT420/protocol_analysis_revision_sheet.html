Medium practice — 15 marks

QUIC video stalls. (a) [5] plan. (b) [5] causes. (c) [5] conclusion.

Answers

(a): endpoint qlog/capture/player metrics. (b): correlate streams, RTT,cwnd,buffer. (c): cite time/metric/confidence. Trap: UDP loss proves cause.

Hard practice — 15 marks

qlog shows at 12.0s RTT=28ms,cwnd=120KB; at 12.4s three losses, cwnd=60KB; at 13.0s stream 6 completes but player buffer is 0. (a) [5] infer. (b) [5] test alternative. (c) [5] remediation experiment.

Answer for every part

(a) Demand / route / worked answer / trap

Route: time-order metrics and distinguish transport from player. Answer: loss followed by cwnd halving indicates congestion response; stream 6 completion means transport recovered, but empty player buffer explains visible stall. Trap: saying stream completion proves no QoE problem.

(b)

Route: seek independent server/player evidence. Answer: correlate server encode/queue spans and segment availability; a late encoder segment could also empty buffer despite good network. Trap: infer encrypted payload content from packets.

(c)

Route: change one measured bottleneck and compare percentiles. Answer: trial lower representation/shorter segment or CDN path, then compare loss, buffer-underflow count and p95 startup/stall over matched sessions. Trap: one anecdotal capture as proof.
