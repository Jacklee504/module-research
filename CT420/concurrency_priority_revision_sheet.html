CT420 Revision

Concurrency and Priority Protocols

Explain blocking precisely, then choose the protocol that bounds or prevents the relevant failure.

Rapid exam refresher

  • Inversion: H waits for L’s lock while M pre-empts L.
  • Inheritance boosts L until release; ceiling restricts lock entry to bound blocking/prevent circular wait.
  • Consistent resource order prevents deadlock; memory locking avoids deadline-critical page faults.

Paper evidence and scope

Priority protocols/semaphores/deadlock recur in 2017/18, 2018/19, 2021/22, 2022/23 and 2024/25; adapted practice.

Medium practice — 15 marks

H waits on L’s mutex while M pre-empts L. (a) [5] inversion. (b) [5] inheritance. (c) [5] ceiling.

Answer for every part

(a): M extends H blocking via L. (b): boost L to H priority until release. (c): ceiling prevents circular wait/bounds blocking. Trap: inheritance prevents every deadlock.

Hard practice — 15 marks

At t=0 L locks S1 for 3 ms. At t=1 H requests S1; at t=1.2 M (medium priority) becomes ready for 5 ms. (a) [5] trace ordinary mutex. (b) [5] trace priority inheritance. (c) [5] two tasks also need S1/S2 in opposite order: give ceiling/order solution.

Answer for every part

(a) Demand / quickest route / worked answer / trap

Route: draw ready/running order by priority. Answer: H blocks at 1; M pre-empts L from 1.2–6.2; L then completes remaining 2 ms and releases S1 at 8.2; H’s lock delay is 7.2 ms. Credit: H→L blocking 2, M pre-emption 2, delay 1. Trap: saying H runs while blocked.

(b)

Route: boost lock holder at H’s request. Answer: at t=1 L inherits H’s priority; M cannot pre-empt L. L runs remaining 2 ms, releases at t=3; H runs then. H blocking is 2 ms, bounded by L’s critical section. Credit: inheritance event 2, corrected timeline 2, bound 1. Trap: boosting M rather than L.

(c)

Route: remove circular wait, then state enforcement. Answer: assign global order S1<S2 and require both tasks acquire S1 then S2; alternatively priority ceiling blocks entry when a higher/equal ceiling resource is held, preventing circular wait. Credit: rule 2, why no cycle 2, protocol link 1. Trap: timeout alone proves prevention.
